## Title

## Description
At TheOneApp Station, every pupil has a character of their very own. But one character steps off the train all grey, and the Gift Shop
is dark. (Psst: every new pupil gets 100 free credits for finishing the tour.)

Here, style is not given. It is earned. Finish a quiz (which one will a magnet attract?), and the credits start to add up, day by day.
Earned, never bought. Then visit the Gift Shop, try things on in the Dressing Room mirror, and keep what you earn. Your character comes
with you into every game: Boss Clash as My Hero, and the Science Claw in Versus against your own pet buddy.

What you learn, you keep. What you earn, you wear.

The only app you need to learn Science: TheOneApp
theoneapp.sg

Featuring Pip, Momo and Basil, the pet buddies, with Heart as narrator.

## Notes for the teacher
- Voices: our own (Heart = Kokoro af_heart; Pip, Momo, Basil = the Character Voice Kit, as in "TheOneApp, Explained"). The earlier films'
  voices are not used.
- Colours: the current v2 cast (Pip grey chinchilla, Momo coral kitten, Basil teal bearded dragon), the explained2/ep20 sprites.
- The pupil's character is drawn by the app's own avatar.js (lab coat, test tube, Safety Goggles: Uncommon, 50 credits, as the real shop
  prices it). The "In the app" panels are real screens of the app (shop.html, bossclash.html "My Hero", claw.html "Vs Pip"), captured from
  a local copy; nothing in the app was changed.
- The science fact: "A magnet attracts things made of iron." (P3 Magnets). Film 2 also used a magnet fact ("A magnet attracts iron."):
  swap it if you prefer a different one (script.py, the cards in scenes.js).
- The credits shown follow the app's rules: a quiz finished +10, an experiment finished +10, up to 30 a day; 50 credits = 2 days.
- No "Next time..." teaser (optional in the brief): add one if a fifth film is planned.
- Changed 24 Nov 2026 (the teacher): Momo now says "Oh wait, why is that one all grey?" (was "Ooh... why..."), and Pip's line is a
  hidden observation, "Does he know he gets 100 free credits for completing the tutorial?" (was "No colour. No style. Not even a hat!"),
  said as a quick, quieter aside. The 100 credits are the app's own welcome gift (tour2.js GIFT = 100, once, when a new pupil finishes
  the tour). Easter egg: a small "Finish the tour +100 credits" poster stands on the platform in that scene; its rim glows as Pip says it.
