# TheOneApp parent ads (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok): research, claims and how they are made (24 Nov 2026)

The teacher asked for ads aimed at **parents**. The four promo films are cartoons made for children and stay "made for kids"; these
ads are a separate, parent-facing cut: a calm narrator (Heart) speaks to the parent, and almost every second shows the **real app**.
Nothing was posted, uploaded or boosted. The copy to paste is in `ADS_COPY.md`.

## 1. What the research found (web search, 24 Nov 2026)

`ads.tiktok.com` cannot be opened from this cloud session (outside its network allowlist), and Meta's help pages were not opened
directly either, so the findings come from the search engine's reading of the official pages plus third-party spec guides. Each row
says which. **Before spending, check the layout once with Meta's safe-zone guardrail in Ads Manager and TikTok's safe-zone template.**

### What works for parent-facing edtech ads

- **Show the product in the first 3 s.** TikTok: over 63 % of the highest-CTR auction ads show the key message or product in the first
  three seconds. Both ads open on the app's real marking screen with the hook already on frame 0.
  (https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/guides/education-marketing-guide via search; https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-ad-creative-best-practices-2026)
- **Problem, then solution; parents want practical information** (TikTok's education guide: several decision-makers, parents need
  practical information; test lengths, CTAs and value propositions). (https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/guides/education-marketing-guide)
- **Native, not glossy; text on screen for sound-off; change only the hook when testing.** (https://searchengineland.com/high-performing-social-ads-curiosity-482756 ; https://motionapp.com/blog/best-dtc-meta-ad-hooks-2025)
- **Social proof only if real.** We have none, so none is used. **Clear CTA** on a still end card.

Files (not in git; `.gitignore`): `ad15_9x16.mp4`, `ad15_4x5.mp4`, `ad30_9x16.mp4`, `ad30_4x5.mp4`: 1080 x 1920 / 1080 x 1350, 30 fps,
H.264 High (CRF 18, BT.709, +faststart), AAC 192 kb/s 48 kHz, -14 LUFS integrated. Checked numbers: section 6.

- **Pictures.** Real app screens only, each with a small "Real app screen" chip, except 2.2 s of promo film 4 (the quiz that pays
  +10 credits), chipped "From our animated film". No child actors, no real faces; the only characters are the app's own (the pupil's
  character, Pip).
- **Marking is real.** `capture_live.js` answered one question on the live app (https://app.theoneapp.sg, P4 "How is a shadow formed?")
  in a fresh phone-sized browser: first answer "The object blocks the light so there is a shadow." → the app's own marking said "Half the
  concept is there", kept "object blocks light", asked about how light travels, and gave the hint; second answer → "Correct and complete",
  2/2. Exactly two calls to `/api/mark`; `/api/log` was **blocked** in that browser, so no row reached the teacher's pupil Google Sheet and
  no session report was mailed. The marking text is saved in `shots/mk_text.json`.
- **Progress Report is real** but from that one demo session (it says "Your child has just started"); it is chipped "Real report · one
  demo session". The top cards only are shown.
- **Other screens** (`capture_local.js`): the local copy of the app served read-only, the character and credits set in browser storage
  (as film 4's `capture.js` did). The School Lab opens only on a tablet or laptop, so the Dark Box Lab and the Mind Map are landscape.
- **Captions.** Burned in, 2-4 words at a time, Fredoka Bold white on a navy outline, key words yellow, from `script.py`. Timed inside
  each recorded line by share of characters (each line is its own file, so a caption is never more than a word or so early or late).
- **Safe zones (9:16).** Every caption and the end card's words sit in x 120-960, y 290-840: inside Meta's zone and TikTok's (rail
  included). The app picture is under the captions (y 530-1250); below it is a blurred fill that the apps' own text may cover.
  `safezones_9x16.jpg` (previews folder) draws both platforms' covered areas over two frames. On TikTok the button rail can sit over
  the right edge of the app picture (picture only, never words).
- **4:5 (Feed).** Captions at the top, the picture in the middle, a small "TheOneApp · theoneapp.sg" footer (feeds put nothing over it).
- **Voice.** Heart, the narrator kit `secondbrain/tools/kits/heart-voice-kit/heart_say.py --style expressive` (voice 3), local model files.
  "TheOneApp" is spoken "The One App", the address "the one app dot S G", "3D" "three D". Every line is checked against
  `explained2/sg_pron.py`; none has a listed word. The buddies' own voices were not used (Heart alone keeps it calm and parent-facing).
- **Music.** Kampong Walk from 0 s, -10 dB alone and -19 dB under Heart (0.25 s duck), 1.4 s fade at the end; then a two-pass loudnorm
  to -14 LUFS, true peak -2 dBTP (AAC adds a little).

## 4. For the teacher to confirm before the ads run

1. **"Try it free".** Is the 7-day free trial switched on in **both** Stripe payment links? The website's price section does not mention
   a trial. If the app goes members-only, the website (and the ad copy) must say how the trial ends (auto-renews at S$9.99/month or
   S$99/year unless cancelled). If there will be no trial, the end card must be re-rendered without "free".
2. **Film 4 in a parent ad** (2.2 s, chipped "From our animated film"). Fine to reuse a clip of a made-for-kids film here?
3. **The live marking capture** used two real `/api/mark` calls on the live app (a few cents of API use); `/api/log` was blocked so the
   pupil sheet is clean. OK to keep the demo answer?
4. **Heart's reading** of "the one app dot S G" and "Primary three to six" (listen to the previews).
5. **CTA button** (`Learn More` now, `Sign Up` later) and whether to put the price in the primary text.
6. TikTok asks advertisers to **label AI-generated content** in some cases; Heart is a synthetic voice. Check the toggle in TikTok Ads
   Manager when uploading (not confirmed from TikTok's page, which could not be opened here).

## 5. Weak spots

- The official spec and policy pages could not be opened from this session; numbers are search readings and third-party measurements.
  Check with the platforms' own safe-zone overlays before spending.
- Only hook A is rendered (B and C are in ADS_COPY.md).
- In 9:16 the bottom third is a blurred fill by design (the apps' own text sits there), so the app picture is 880 x 720: app text is
  small but readable on a phone.
- 30 fps (not 60): the pictures are screens and pans, so 60 adds file size and nothing visible.
- Captions are timed by character share, not word-aligned.
- No social proof exists yet; when real parents say something (with permission), it can be added, quoted exactly.
