**Preface**
The **Inspiring Science** series is written based on the Primary Science Syllabus
2023 by the Ministry of Education, Singapore. This series provides opportunities
for students to understand the ways of thinking and doing Science.
This textbook comprises different themes to help students appreciate connections
between life and physical sciences. This encourages coherence and integration of
scientific ideas and makes the learning of Science meaningful for students.
Students explore Science by thinking and doing, and by understanding themselves
and the world around them through real-world contexts.
We hope that the Inspiring Science series will be a trusted companion for
students in their four-year journey of Science learning in primary school and lay
a strong foundation that will spark their curiosity for Science learning in future.
