## Textbook coverage

- [x] Three main parts and their functions (leaves, stem, roots)
- [x] Leaves are green because a green substance absorbs light to make food
- [x] Tiny openings in leaves let gases in and out
- [x] The stem holds the plant upright so the leaves get light, and transports water, mineral salts and food
- [x] Roots anchor the plant and absorb water and mineral salts
- [x] Exam trap: roots do not absorb food
- [x] Data reasoning: fewer leaves → less food → less growth (based on the textbook's bar-graph question, in my own words)
- [x] Singapore examples: kai lan and nai bai (leaves we eat); sweet potato and carrot (storage roots)
