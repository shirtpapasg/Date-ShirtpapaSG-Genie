In this episode you'll learn:
• Good conductors of heat (metals) let heat flow through quickly
• Poor conductors of heat (glass, plastic, wood) let heat flow through slowly
• Pots are metal so food heats quickly, but handles are plastic or wood so they stay cool
• Air is a poor conductor: wool and polystyrene trap air to slow heat flow
