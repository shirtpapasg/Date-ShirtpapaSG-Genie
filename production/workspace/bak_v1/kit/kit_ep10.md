In this episode you'll learn:
• Mass is measured in kilograms (kg) and grams (g); 1 kg = 1000 g
• Use an electronic balance on a flat, steady surface, switch it on with the pan empty, and check it reads zero (press tare if there is a zero error)
• Mass of a liquid or gas = mass of container with contents − mass of empty container
• A pumped-up ball (597 g) minus the flat ball (586 g) shows the air inside has a mass of 11 g
