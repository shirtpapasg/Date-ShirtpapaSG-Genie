## How each episode is built
- `$S/epN/epNb.html` is the rendered page (1920x1080 SVG; `window.render(t)` draws time t seconds).
- It is GENERATED by `python3 $S/prep_all.py N` from the pristine source `$S/bak_v1/epN/epN.html`
  (for N=2: `$S/bak_v1/ep2/ep2_newplant.html`). Never edit epN.html / epNb.html / bak_v1 by hand.
- Your fixes go in `$S/fixes/epN.json`: a JSON list of `[old, new]` string pairs. Each `old` must occur
  EXACTLY ONCE in the source scene code (copy it from `$S/bak_v1/epN/epN.html`; for EP04/EP14/EP02/EP03/EP06/EP18
  some strings were already rewritten by prep_all's HTMLFIX, so check the current `$S/epN/epNb.html` too and
  use text that exists in both, or the post-HTMLFIX version). Prefer small, local edits: change x/y numbers in
  `popAt(...)`, `translate(...)`, `tf(...)`, `set(e, "x", ...)`, chip font size (`{fs: ..}`), or shorten a label.
  After editing the JSON, re-run `python3 $S/prep_all.py N` (takes ~30 s; it asserts every fix matched once).
- The intro scene ("welcome", the TODAY YOU'LL LEARN board) and the goodbye are shared code; do not change them.

## Tools
- Audit: `node $S/audit.js $S/epN/epNb.html $S/audit/epN.json` — flags kinds: out-of-box, offscreen,
  under-caption, under-keyword, text-overlap, text-on-drawing (each lasting >= 0.5 s), with time ranges.
- Flag sheet: `node $S/sheet.js $S/epN/epNb.html $S/audit/epN.json OUT.jpg` — one frame per flag, offending
  text boxed red (other item blue), titled "#i kind @time". Look at it with the Read tool.
- Single frames: `node /tmp/claude-0/-home-user/8764a423-5385-5445-b38a-c2c2633ecdb7/scratchpad/snap.js PAGE OUTPREFIX t1 t2 ...`
  writes OUTPREFIX_t.jpg (1920x1080). Combine frames into grids with python PIL to save reading effort.
- Timeline: `$S/epN/tl.json` has `scenes` (t0/t1), `segs`, `cues[segId][word]` (time the word is spoken).
- Put your images in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/8764a423-5385-5445-b38a-c2c2633ecdb7/scratchpad/snaps/` (any name prefixed with your episode).

## What to do for each episode
1. Run the audit and the flag sheet. Decide for each flag: REAL problem or acceptable.
   Acceptable (leave alone): a label sitting fully inside its own pill/bubble even if that pill is a path; mind-map
   branch lines that end at/under their own labels; an emoji deliberately placed on a body (e.g. ✋ on chest, ❤️ in chest);
   a word or chip that passes over something for < 0.5 s while animating in; text intentionally inside a panel/card.
   REAL: words on top of a drawing (body, arm, skeleton, plant, tube, parchment edge...), words touching/overlapping
   other words or emoji, big numbers or labels over the top-left episode tag (tag occupies x 40-470, y 34-92),
   anything in the caption band (y > 965) or under the KEY WORDS shelf (top right, x > 1500, y < ~ 120 + 56*number of keywords),
   text outside its box, text off screen (keep a 40 px safe margin).
2. ALSO check by eye what the audit cannot see: make a grid of frames every ~5 s across the whole episode
   (e.g. 12 frames per 3x4 grid image, each frame resized to 640x360) and look for any overlap or crowding.
3. Fix real problems with entries in fixes/epN.json: move labels/chips beside the drawing (not on it), move big
   step numbers down below the tag (e.g. y >= 140) or to a free area, reduce a chip font slightly if needed.
   Keep the look and style. Do NOT change narration, timing, cue words, colours or scene order.
4. Re-run prep_all + audit + sheet, and look at the fixed frames. Repeat until every remaining flag is
   genuinely acceptable.
