## Facts to confirm before approving (rule 5)
1. Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia CEO on 23 Nov 2026, about 11 days after stepping down: Nvidia's 8-K filing (SEC) and NBC News agree.
2. "More than two hundred billion switches": Nvidia's Blackwell GPU is reported at about 208 billion transistors. To confirm on Nvidia's own page; the safe wording is "more than a hundred billion".
3. Singapore makes about one in every ten of the world's chips: EDB (edb.gov.sg) and SingStat both say about 10%.
4. Nvidia designs its chips in the US; the newest are made by TSMC in Taiwan (widely reported).
5. Clean rooms cleaner than an operating theatre; a silicon crystal "as long as a child is tall" (typically 1–2 m); wafers thinner than a coin (about 0.8 mm): standard industry figures, to confirm.
6. Last week's answer (compost heat from decomposers) matches EP10.
