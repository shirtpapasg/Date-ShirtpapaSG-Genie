In this episode you'll learn:
• Volume is the amount of space something takes up, measured in cm³ or ml (1 cm³ = 1 ml)
• A measuring cylinder is more accurate than a beaker
• Place the cylinder on a flat surface and read at eye level from the bottom of the curved surface
• A solid's volume = new water level − first water level (60 − 50 = 10 cm³)
