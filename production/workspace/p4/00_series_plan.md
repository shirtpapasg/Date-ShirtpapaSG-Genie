# Primary 4 Science, Explained — Series Plan

**Source:** Inspiring Science Primary 4 Textbook (MOE / Marshall Cavendish, 2024; Primary Science Syllabus 2023).

**How the book is used:** the textbook is used only as a map of topics, concepts, key terms and common mistakes. Every script is original writing: no textbook sentences, questions or pictures are copied.

**Audience:**
- Singapore P4 students (ages 9–10), and the parents helping them.
- US Grade 3–5 students.
- Both audiences watch the same video. Each script includes a short **US bridge** (°F, US examples and settings) and a **Singapore anchor** (local places, food and habits).

**Format:**
- 4–6 minutes, visually styled like the haze video: word-by-word captions, a pop-up on every key word, maps and diagrams, and a visual change every 2–3 seconds.
- Each episode ends with one ask only.

## Narrators: alternate every episode

The voice **swaps after each video**:
- **Odd episodes (1, 3, 5 … 21): Heart.** American, female, warm (Kokoro voice `af_heart`, accent `en-us`).
- **Even episodes (2, 4, 6 … 22): George.** British, male (Kokoro voice `bm_george`, accent `en-gb`).

Why alternate:
- Both audiences hear a familiar accent regularly.
- Each video feels fresh.
- The channel gets two recognisable "hosts".

Rules:
- Each episode uses ONE narrator from start to finish; the voice does not swap mid-video.
- If episodes are released in a different order (see below), keep the swap by **release order**, not episode number, so viewers never hear the same voice twice in a row. Record the final narrator in each script's header.
- Both voices are free and open-source (Kokoro-82M, Apache-2.0), so they can be used in monetised videos.

## Release order suggestion

- Follow the school term: **Matter (8–12) → Systems (1–7) → Light and Shadows (13–17) → Heat (18–22)**.
- Or release the most "clickable" episodes first to grow the channel: **14, 20, 8, 7, 16**.

## What every script contains

1. Two hooks, each scored by `hookscore.py`.
2. The script, beat by beat, with `[ON SCREEN: …]` on every beat (keyword-triggered visuals).
3. A runtime estimate.
4. A **Textbook coverage checklist** (concepts and key terms).
5. **Exam wording box:** the exact phrasing that earns marks in Singapore exams.
6. **Misconceptions busted.**
7. **US bridge** and **Singapore anchor** notes.
8. One closing ask.
