## Textbook coverage checklist

- [x] A system is made of parts, each with its own job (function).
- [x] The parts interact or work together to perform a function.
- [x] If one part fails, the whole system is likely to be affected (bike-part prediction, original version).
- [x] Students name other everyday systems (train line, soccer team).
- [x] Preview of the human systems (respiratory, circulatory, skeletal, muscular, digestive). Full detail comes in Episodes 5–7.
- [x] Preview of the plant system (roots, stem, leaves). Full detail comes in Episodes 2–4.

## Misconceptions addressed

- "Each part works alone": shown wrong by the pedal → chain → wheel sequence.
- "Losing any part stops everything": the bell shows that some parts affect safety rather than function, so the textbook's wording is *likely* or *may* be affected.
