In this episode you'll learn:
• Temperature is a measure of how hot or cold something is, in degrees Celsius (°C)
• Our sense of touch cannot measure temperature accurately
• Digital thermometers measure body temperature, infrared thermometers measure surfaces, and lab thermometers measure liquids and gases
• Lab thermometer: bulb fully in, wait until it stops moving, read at eye level
