## What this suggests

1. **EP1 is good because you reviewed it scene by scene.** About 20 of the 45 notes were visual: buddy art, floating objects, proportions, readability, cuteness. No episode since has had that review.
2. **Autopilot keeps the rules, but not your eye.** The rules from EP1 were written down and are followed. But "is Basil cute here?", "does this scene have focus?" and "is this object floating?" can only be caught by watching.
3. A short check before upload would bring EP1's level back without slowing everything down. For example, you see 6–8 key stills or a 60-second preview of each part, and give notes like you did for EP1.
