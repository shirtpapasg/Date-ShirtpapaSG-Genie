## How the engine is set up for you

- **Load order** (page.html): kit/style.js, tl, sprites, lib.js, props.js, hotel.js, garden.js, trail.js, **materials.js**, common.js, shrink.js, p1.js, p2.js, sound_cues.js, main.js. - **Key words (shelf)** are drawn by lib.js `shelf()`: Part 1 OBJECT, MATERIAL, CERAMIC, CLAY; Part 2 RUBBER, SAP, FABRIC. A new term pops large for `KWHOLD` = 2.0 s (was 1.5), then flies to the shelf. SAP has `o.at` (pops beside the Mark Sentence card, no dimming).
- **Material names** (GLASS, METAL, PLASTIC, WOOD) pop at their `nm_*` mark: placeholder drawing `namePop()` in materials.js (stamp-coloured pill, 2.0 s, then a low hop to `BOARD_AT(k, t, s)`). Set `window.BOARD_AT` to where the tile is in each scene, or redraw the pop. CERAMIC, RUBBER, FABRIC are shelf words; their copy hops to the tile (`NAMES` entries with `shelf: true`; the sound already plays a hop at `th + .7`).
- **The board's data** is `MAT` in materials.js (book order, stamp colours, source-slot words). `tileFull(k, t)` says whether a tile is full (Part 2 starts with METAL, CERAMIC, GLASS, PLASTIC full; `tile_*` marks fill the rest).
- **Holds** (already in the timeline): new term 2.0 s + Heart waits ≥ 0.6 s; Mark Sentence cards 2.5 s after the last word; cream book strips ≥ 3.5 s from the first word (and ≥ 0.6 s after the last); the furnace word strip has its line's time plus the next lines (journey); the kiln strip 3.0 s after "kiln" plus the bowl hop (`bowlback` + 1.0 s).
- **Sound** is finished for these timelines (sound_cues.js -> cues_p*.json -> song.js -> out/mix_p*.wav). If you move a beat, change its cue in sound_cues.js, not a number in song.js. New sound kinds used: `sfx` (a library file), `boing`, `tok`, `bubble`, `pomf`, `cloth`, `baa`, `twang`; ambience region `R.canteen`.
- **Scores** are pushed in p1.js / p2.js (`SCORE`). The lizard: Part 1 on a classroom window during Round 2's ring; Part 2 under the wooden bench during the Showdown's ring.
- **Buddies are never drawn in code**: sprites only, in aura discs, via `buddy()`.
