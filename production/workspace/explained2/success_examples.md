# Success examples the teacher shared (19 Nov 2026): "success examples for us to not reinvent the wheels"
Learn principles only; never copy characters, names, music, footage or look.

1. Sesame Street, "Elmo Visits the Farm on Sesame Street! | THREE Sesame Street Full Episodes"
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GOSF1qlBO54
   - characters look at the viewer and ask for help; a field trip to a real place full of things to discover; many short segments of
     different kinds; rhythm/song as a memory hook; warmth; long multi-episode compilations next to single episodes and Shorts.
2. Dr. Plants, "World's Biggest vs Smallest Egg - Hatching Experiment"  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BthfXVCRWEQ
3. Dr. Plants, "Simulating Africa for 100 Days"  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3oT3-4sSOY
   - an extreme, simple challenge premise in the title ("X vs Y", "for 100 days"); a mini-world in a box that changes over time;
     a big on-screen day counter and time-lapse; living things as the characters; stakes and suspense ("will it hatch? will it
     survive?"); twists along the way; the payoff held to the end; cinematic close-ups.
   - For us: a "Day 1 ... Day 30" experiment box fits our lock-in game (predict what will happen by day 30, then watch), and suits
     topics like living vs non-living, life cycles, plant needs, germination, decomposition, food chains.
