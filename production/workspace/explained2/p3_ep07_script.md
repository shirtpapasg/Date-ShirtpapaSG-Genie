## 2. The spine and the split

One kitchen table in an HDB flat. On it, on Day 1: a slice of bread, a clear resealable bag, and a bowl of bread dough with a packet of yeast. EP6 asked which of the two fungi is useful, mould or yeast. **Part 1** seals the slice in the bag and watches ten days pass. The fuzzy patches are the first surprise; Momo wants to open the bag (HOLD ON!). Nobody fed the mould, so how does it get food? The child becomes a spore, lands on the bread on Day 1 before the bag closes, and lives the book's definition from the inside. Then a mushroom from the market shows where spores are kept, and the dare is answered: yeast is useful, bread mould is harmful, and there are other useful fungi. On Day 10 an adult bins the bag, still shut, and washes their hands. "But they look clean!" **Part 2** answers that: a microscope, the three shapes, and then the child becomes one bacterium on a fingertip at recess and is washed away with soap. Are all bacteria harmful? No: the yoghurt in the fridge. Then the exam skill the plan puts here (read a table of clues), the chapter's map (the four groups with everything EP4 to EP7 put under them), the chant, the Exam corner, and the dare for Chapter 3.

**Why the split is here:** each part has one group and one question. Part 1 can be seen with the eyes (mould, mushrooms, dough). Part 2 needs a microscope, which is itself the book's link between the two groups ("even smaller than fungi"), and it has room for the chapter's close.

## 3. Cast, the game and the conventions

- **The game:** cream answer cards with big numbers; fingers 1, 2, 3; arms crossed for HOLD ON!; one finger per point on the score hand. The buddies score a point on a lock-in round when two of the three are right; nothing in HOLD ON!; nothing in a Showdown built on a buddy's mistake. **Scores:** Part 1: HOLD ON! 1 : 0, Round 1 2 : 1, Round 2 3 : 2 (You 3, Buddies 2). Part 2: Round 1 1 : 1, Showdown 3 : 1 (You 3, Buddies 1).
- **Colours.** The two trays: **USEFUL** green, **HARMFUL** blue (the rulebook's two fixed answer colours). The chapter map uses EP4's crate colours: PLANTS green, ANIMALS orange, FUNGI brown, BACTERIA blue. No buddy colour and no sun yellow is used for an answer.
- **The bag** must read clearly as what it is (teacher's note 1): a clear resealable bag with a coloured zip strip, lying flat on a white tray with the label **OUR BREAD BAG**, on the table, with a shadow. A day pill (**DAY 1** ... **DAY 10**) stands beside the tray. Nobody lifts the bag until the adult's gloved hand carries it low to the bin at the end.
- **Things do not float.** Trays, boards, easels and the microscope stand on the table or the floor, or slide low with dust puffs. The only things that drift are the spores, inside the journey and from the mushroom's gills, because that is the science.
- **Realism (brief):** bread mould is fuzzy patches (white first, then green, then grey-black), never a flat stain; it starts as small dots and spreads. The mushroom has a cap on top, gills in thin lines under the cap, a stalk below; its spores are a fine dust from the gills. Dough is bigger after the yeast has worked. Tempeh is a white-coated slab with soya beans showing. Bacteria are rods, spheres and spirals only, with no faces, eyes, arms or legs, in the microscope view and in the journey. The microscope has an eyepiece, a lens and a stage. The hands are a child's hands; no face is shown.
- **New art the builder needs:** the HDB kitchen set (table by the window, sink with a tap and a soap pump, fridge with magnets, wall clock, a pedal bin; a morning version and the same light for Day 10); the tray with the bag (empty, with a fresh slice, and six mould stages); the dough bowl with a cloth (flat, risen) and a yeast packet; the spore-journey world (kitchen air with giant far-off cups, the bread's holes like caves, the bag's plastic sky, white threads, crumbs breaking into small dots, a fuzzy patch with tiny stalks and dust); a market mushroom (side view and underside), a punnet of mushrooms, the tempeh board with a nasi padang stall behind, a mouldy slice board; the gloved adult hand and the bin; the hands at the sink (dry, soapy, rinsing); the microscope board with its round view; the fingertip-journey world (fingerprint ridges like hills, rods, spheres and spirals around, the canteen far away and huge, a sandwich like a cliff, soap bubbles, the drain); the yoghurt cup and a yoghurt drink; Pip's table on an easel; the chapter map board; the three dare boards (plastic snack box, metal snack box, glass jar: shared with EP8) and a pile of sand. Reused: the frog, kingfisher, fish, ant, monitor lizard and bat (EP5, EP6), the chilli plant and bird's nest fern (EP4), EP6's two dare boards (mouldy slice, loaf with a yeast packet), the house lizard, the rules scene, the ring, cards, coins, stamps, the ending.
