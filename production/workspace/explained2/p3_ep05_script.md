## 2. The spine and the split

One morning on the boardwalk. The spotter card has three empty circles. **Part 1** fills the first: the frog. On the way it teaches what an animal is (head, body, limbs; cannot make its own food), where animals live, that coverings differ, and that scientists group animals by observable characteristics. The frog is first because it is the hardest of the three to place: it swims, it lays eggs in water, and children call it "a kind of fish" or "a kind of lizard". The child becomes the frog and feels both homes. **Part 2** fills the other two circles: the kingfisher (and the penguin, which cannot fly) and the fish; then the animal from the cliffhanger, the mudskipper, which lives on land AND in water and is still a fish. The child becomes the mudskipper and feels the tide go out. The Showdown adds the sea star. The dare hands over to Episode 6 with the three groups still to come and one question that needs only counting.

## 3. Cast, the game and the conventions

As in EP1 to EP3: Heart narrates (Kokoro af_heart, speed 0.95, about 0.45 s between sentences; 0.9 in the two journeys). Pip is careful, Basil is bold, Momo is curious; each speaks in their Character Voice Kit voice, about 7% slower. Choices use the cream answer cards with big numbers; the viewer answers with fingers 1, 2, 3; arms crossed for HOLD ON!; one finger per point on the score hand. Scores: Part 1 You 3, Buddies 2. Part 2 You 4, Buddies 2. The three stamp colours on the spotter card are green (amphibian), orange (bird) and blue (fish); they are stamp colours only, not answer colours.
