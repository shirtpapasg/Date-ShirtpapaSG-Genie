## Read first: the standing rules (all from `../SERIES_FORMAT.md`, 22 Nov; EP14's `SCENES_TODO.md` rules 1-14 apply unchanged)

1. **No aura discs; buddies IN the scene, BIG** (1d): contact shadow, the scene's light on them; the speaker 45-55% of the frame height,
   every other buddy on screen at least h 330; balanced layouts (speaker one side, the others the other side, the prop between).
2. **The camera frames whoever talks and pushes in on what is named** (the seed, the pot's window, the journal, the ruler, the board, the
   homework, the jars), then eases back; never a jump cut inside a place.
3. **Talking = a moving mouth**: a speaking buddy is ALWAYS in its talking sprite (lip-sync, blinks) and pops back to its pose after the line.
   Listeners: only ONE reacts to a line with a pose, and only when the line gives a reason (a question: thinking; a problem: oops /
   surprised; something learnt: cheer); otherwise a small nod. No buddy swaps pose more than once in 6 s unless staged here (lib.js
   `reactPose`, `learnAt`).
4. **Reactions follow the answer** (lib.js `OUTCOMES`, set for EP15): Part 1 Round 1 Basil right / Momo, Pip wrong; HOLD ON Pip right / Basil
   wrong; Part 2 Round 1 Basil, Momo right / Pip wrong; Showdown Basil, Pip right / Momo wrong. Wrong = shy (oops), right = confident smile;
   hands up only when something is learnt. Only a buddy who got it right explains the right answer (the script already does).
5. **Look-closers**: the thing fills the panel, each named part its own push-in and big label, one buddy FACE in a lower corner reacting;
   no ghost overlaps; one verdict at its `v_*` mark held 2.5 s. **Countdowns take the stage** (ring half the frame, clue labels >= 40 px on
   top of the ring, blue stage, one big buddy). **ONE set of big numbered picture cards** per lock-in.
6. **The hidden animal moves** to a new fair spot in every scene (listed per scene below), is named first, revealed in its black-ringed
   circle on the blue stage, and the reveal ends with the buddy's fun fact (wired `funFact`: draw its pictures).
7. **The journeys bring company**: a *You: a ...* tag and the viewer's own presence (the seed coat / the petals at the frame's edge), the
   three buddies shrunk riding along (`riders`), reacting at each stop, never covering the view.
8. **Poses in colour** (prep_sprites.py: Momo coral, Pip grey, Basil teal): Pip and Momo: sit, cheer, oops, surprised, arms_crossed, pointing,
   thinking, waving; Basil: the same but NO arms_crossed (lib.js `POSE_SUB` swaps it to thinking). Back views: `poses/momo_back.png`,
   `poses/basil_back.png` (not in poses.json yet; add them if used, never speaking).
9. **Things never float**; an adult's hands (four fingers and a thumb) do the knife, the planting, the water; every sample or picture is
   labelled; every tag points to what it describes; labels inside the frame; nothing in the bottom fifth at the end.
10. **Every place sounds alive** (9): sound_cues.js is a GENERIC Stage 1 plan (rituals and beds); add each place's bed and the foley ON the
   marks (listed at the top of sound_cues.js).
