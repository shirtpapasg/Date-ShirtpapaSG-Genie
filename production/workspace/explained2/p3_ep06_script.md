## 2. The spine and the split

One day at the Singapore Botanic Gardens, with the six-tile board from EP5's dare. **Part 1** (morning to dusk by the lake) starts where EP5 stopped: the ant and the spider. The child counts legs (six, then eight), learns the insect's body plan and that wings are not the test (HOLD ON! with a pigeon), and then meets the monitor lizard on the bank: a reptile, like Basil. The child becomes one of its scales and feels its warmth follow the shade, the sun and the lake, before the book's word "cold-blooded" names it. At dusk, dark winged shapes leave the palms: is it a bird? **Part 2** (dusk in Palm Valley) answers it: the child becomes a hair on a fruit bat's back, flies out into the cool night and stays warm. A bat is a mammal: hair or fur, milk, warm-blooded. Then the exam skills the plan puts here: compare two animals with a reason (bat and bird), find the odd one out (cat, monkey, lizard), and read all the clues (the mystery creature, a sea turtle). The board is full; the chant gives one clue per group; the Exam corner gives the chapter's takeaway; the dare hands over to Episode 7.

**Why the split is here:** Part 1 holds the two groups that are spotted by counting and touching (legs; dry, scaly skin) and ends on the idea it has just taught (wings are not the clue). Part 2 holds the mammal, whose clue (fur) is hidden in the dark at the cliffhanger, and the two exam skills that need all six groups.

- **The game:** cream answer cards with big numbers; fingers 1, 2, 3; arms crossed for HOLD ON!; one finger per point on the score hand. The buddies score a point on a lock-in round when two of the three are right; they score nothing in HOLD ON! and nothing in a Showdown built on a buddy's mistake. **Scores:** Part 1: Round 1 1 : 1, HOLD ON! 2 : 1, Round 2 3 : 2 (You 3, Buddies 2). Part 2: Round 1 1 : 1, Round 2 2 : 2, Showdown 4 : 2 (You 4, Buddies 2).
- **The six-tile board** (EP5's dare, reused): SIX ANIMAL GROUPS, tiles in the book's order AMPHIBIANS, BIRDS, FISH, INSECTS, REPTILES, MAMMALS. EP5's stamps stay (green, orange, blue). New stamp colours, stamp colours only, never answer colours: INSECTS chestnut #8B4A2B, REPTILES slate #5E6B73, MAMMALS plum-rose #B5476B (none is a buddy colour, green, blue or sun yellow).
- **Things do not float.** The board, easels and picture boards stand on the ground or slide low with dust puffs; the lizard picture in Round 2 slides out low along the ground; the magnifier and thermometers stand on little feet.
- **Thermometers:** a plain glass tube with a red column, no numbers, no arrowheads; plain label lines only (brief, realism notes).
- **Realism (brief):** an insect's six legs all join the thorax (the middle part), its feelers are on the head, its abdomen is in segments, its wings (if any) join the thorax. The spider has eight legs, two body parts and no feelers. The snake has no legs; the monitor lizard, the crocodile and the turtle have four; the sea turtle has flippers and a shell. The bat has fur on its body and thin skin wings; the monkey has a tail; the mother cat lies on her side to feed her kittens. The cockroach is not shown.

Reused: the six-tile board, the kingfisher (EP5), the house lizard, the rules scene, the ring, cards, coins, stamps, the ending.
