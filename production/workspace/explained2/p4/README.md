## 1. The shape of every P4 script
Each script has the same sections, so any agent can find anything:
- **Header table:** format, topic (with the Inspiring Science P4 pages), big idea, length, spoken words, words a minute, voice-on share and
  words by speaker, narrator, guest, the Heart mystery hint (or "none, on purpose"), the key words and who teaches each, the old video it
  replaces (never deleted).
- **0. How this episode does the new P4 style** (a table against the teacher's 25 Nov direction), what is kept from P3 and what is left out.
- **1. The chain of questions** (7-9 lines; each answer opens the next question; the last line is the link into the next episode).
- **2. Science checks** (every picture and every sentence; the book's own sentences quoted; what is P5/P6 and kept out).
- **3. The music plan** (which cue goes where).
- **4. The beat table:** Time · Line id · Who · Exact words · On screen · camera · Buddies (pose: reason) · Music · SFX. A bold row opens each
  scene (its length, set, camera, buddy layout and bed); *hold* rows are silent action and think time.
- **5. Who is right, who is wrong** (lib.js OUTCOMES) and every strong pose with its reason.
- **6. Realism notes** for the drawing agent and the teacher's-eye review.
- **7. The words that earn marks**, the exam wording ("the way accepted in PSLE"), the misconceptions busted (each with where and how it is
  answered), the non-textbook sentences, the US bridge (only if natural) and the Singapore anchors.
- **8. Three Shorts.** **9. YouTube** (title, thumbnail, description, tags). **10. App moments** (real `videos.js` keys).
- **11. Teacher sheet.** **12. For the teacher to check** (science questions first, each with what the script does now; then other
  questions with a recommendation). **13. The Heart mystery log row.**

## 2. The story (every episode)
- **The problem is the first line** (a voice in the first second) and the whole problem is on screen inside 20 s. No introductions, no goal
  cards, no chant.
- **Three fix cards**, one set, big and numbered (each a picture card with its number badge; the cards carry the idea, not a wrong fact as a
  statement). Heart: "...Show me one, two or three fingers!" → **the lock-in** (6 s ring, the countdown takes the stage, ticks speeding up,
  bell, LOCKED IN!) → **the picks**: each buddy stands under their own card with a one-line reason.
- **The spine:** problem → fix 1 fails (that failure IS the misconception) → (fix 2 fails: a second misconception or a near-miss) →
  LOOK CLOSER (the why, with a picture model) → twist / second try → the real solution, TESTED (a real test the child could do: the
  mechanic's wheel test, the water test, a side-by-side match, a time-lapse with a day counter) → the words that earn marks → callback joke.
- **The BE THE THING journey** stays in every episode (quiet, micro-world pad only, YOU tag, the buddies riding along in back views).
- **Think time:** the lock-in, plus at least one more silent think pause (2.5-3.5 s) on a question that matters.
- **A villain from EP2 on (teacher, 27 Nov 2026: "for episode 2 onwards, include a villain"):** one of the approved troublemakers (Kopi the
  Macaque, GLITCH the Robot, Duchess) causes or worsens the problem in a way that IS the science, stays funny and never truly mean or
  scary, is won over by that episode's scientific method (never beaten by force), and leaves a hook to return. EP2: GLITCH.
- **Episodes link by story:** EP1 ends with Chilli hopping up to the new Science Help Desk → EP2; EP2 ends with a leaf that is not Chilli's
  → EP3; EP3 ends with Spike at the desk → EP4; EP4 teases EP5's obstacle race (the Challenge format).
- **The Science Help Desk** (a folding table with a desk bell at the void deck) is the buddies' P4 club corner: the home of every Help Desk
  (D) episode and the place a Case File (A) starts.

## 4. Pacing (the teacher: "the rhythm of P3 EP1-10")
- **Target:** about 105-117 spoken words a minute; voice on about 70% of the time; real think time on every choice. The four scripts:
  112, 110, 113 and 115 words a minute; voice on 73-75% (estimates).
- **The estimate model** (the same in all four): Heart about 2.9 words a second (2.6 in look-closers and journeys), buddies and guests 2.7,
  all three together 2.2; +0.35 s per extra sentence in a line; 0.55 s between lines and 1.0 s between scenes (P4 style guide); +2.0 s for
  a key-word pop; the lock-in ring 6.5 s; holds as written. The pilot EP27 ran at 139 words a minute with the voice on 79%: too fast for
  the teacher's taste, so P4 goes back to P3 EP1-10's pace (Heart speed 0.90, 0.5 s between sentences; buddies 12% slower).
- **At build:** if the timeline shows more than about 72% voice, lengthen the holds and think pauses (never add words); if a part runs
  long, cut chatter first (each script lists what to cut), never the look-closers, think pauses or the card.
- **About 6 minutes, one video,** like the pilot (6:09-6:43 including about 45 s of closing rituals and the blooper). Each script says why
  it is not two parts.

## 5. Humour by age
- **EP1-EP3:** a bit of slapstick, light (EP1: Pip's slow topple on a bike that cannot move, the rope on Basil's head; EP2: a leaf on the
  desk bell, water on Basil's feet, the ants; EP3: the oversized detective cap, the pandan leaf over Pip's head). Nobody is hurt; each buddy
  laughs at himself.
- **From EP4: no slapstick.** Wit, running gags, arguments with evidence ("Right answer, wrong reason. No point." / "I'd like to see the
  replay."), and a blooper that is a line, not a fall.
- **Running gags:** Pip's "I've got a plan!" ("That's how this started."); Basil's calm one-liners (rimshot); Momo's snack emergencies (the
  curry puff in EP2, "Snack emergency, solved." in EP4); the slow-motion "Let's look at that again" replay of a mistake (EP1-EP3, and as a
  joke in EP4); a short blooper after the end card. Callbacks across episodes: Pip's bike (EP2), Chilli's roots (EP4), the rain tree (EP1,
  EP4), Basil's "cucumber".
- Never mean, never babyish: jokes are about the idea, and the wrong buddy is always right about something later.

  per scene, revealed with a true fun fact). Fix-It, Help Desk and Challenge episodes leave it out.

## 8. Words, science and the book
- **The textbook (Inspiring Science P4, 2024) is a map only:** every line is original; the book's sentences are quoted in each script's
  science checks for the teacher, and the "words that earn marks" card uses the book's terms in the form a P4 pupil writes in an exam.
- **Book terms, not the brief's:** "net veins / net pattern" (not "network veins"), "straight edge / jagged edge", "spine-like leaves",
  "storage roots", "stem cutting", "woody / non-woody stem", "absorb water and mineral salts", "transports water, mineral salts and food".
- **Positive language** (P4 style guide): "superpowers", not "cheat"; "wilting", not "dying"; "loves me too much", not "loved to death".
- **Level gate:** no P5/P6 words (photosynthesis, chlorophyll, stomata, xylem, phloem, root hair, oxygen, starch, transpiration).
- **Every wrong idea is a buddy's (or a guest's family's), answered within seconds** (section 7 tables give where and how).
- **Pictures never plant a misconception:** a bike chain drawn on the right gears; climbers that twine their stems (the butterfly pea),
  not tendrils, when the line says "coils"; roots white when healthy, brown when rotting; comparisons side by side, never overlapped.
