# Pet buddies: the painting brief for Gemini (version 2, 23 Nov 2026)

One art style for all three buddies: **Pip's soft painted style, with a slightly thicker outline**. Momo (coral) and Basil (teal) are
repainted in it. Pip stays as he is.

---

## How to work in Gemini (every picture)

1. Start a new chat for each buddy. Attach two references:
   - `pip_sit_closed.png` (the STYLE to copy)
   - the buddy's current picture (its COLOURS and BODY): Momo: `momo_c_talk_closed.jpg`; Basil: `basil_c_sit_closed.jpg`
2. Paste the **STYLE BLOCK**, then the buddy's **CHARACTER BLOCK**.
3. Ask for **one picture at a time** with its **pose line** (below).
4. For a **talking set** (4 pictures): make picture 1, then for 2, 3 and 4 say:
   *"Same picture exactly. Do not move anything. Change ONLY the mouth to …"* (or the eyes).
5. If a picture drifts (thin or brown line, flat colours, a different face), reply:
   *"Match the chinchilla reference's outline and soft shading exactly, and keep this character's body from its own reference."*
6. Save each picture separately: 1024 x 1024, one buddy, white background. Send them to me one per message.

---

## STYLE BLOCK (paste once per chat)

> Draw a cute 2D children's cartoon character for an educational science video for 9-year-olds.
> Style: soft painted digital illustration, like a premium children's storybook. Gentle soft shading and soft, fluffy painted fur
> (or soft painted scales for the lizard). A clean, smooth, medium-thick dark navy outline (#1F2A44) around the whole character and
> its main shapes: a little bolder than a thin sketch line, but not a thick sticker outline. The same line thickness everywhere.
> Big, round, glossy dark eyes with a white highlight. Friendly, warm and expressive face.
> Not 3D, not a photo, not watercolour, not flat vector, no texture noise.
> Composition: ONE character only, full body, sitting, centred, filling about 85% of the image height, with a small white margin on
> every side. Plain pure white background. No ground shadow, no text, no labels, no frame, no props.
> Image: square, 1024 x 1024.
> Match the reference picture's style (the grey chinchilla) exactly; keep the character's own colours and body from its own reference.

## CHARACTER BLOCKS

**MOMO** (her new style test was right: keep it)
> Momo is a chubby, round kitten. Coral-orange fur (#FB7158) with darker coral-red tabby stripes on the head, back, legs and tail;
> cream muzzle, chest, belly, paw tips and tail tip; pink inner ears, a small pink nose and pink paw pads. A big round head, short legs
> and a fluffy tail. Big round dark eyes with a white highlight, short eyelashes. Keep exactly this look in every picture.

**BASIL** (fix: he drifted into an iguana in the test)
> Basil is a cute BABY BEARDED DRAGON, not an iguana. Keep Basil's exact body shape from his reference picture: chubby and round,
> sitting, with a big round head and a short snout. Big round DARK eyes with a white highlight (not green, not grey, not small).
> A large fluffy cream beard that frames his face, a cream throat and belly. Soft teal-grey scales. Bright teal (#14B8A6) spikes ONLY
> on the top of his head and the top of his back (not all down the back). A curled tail with bright teal bands. A long, friendly smile.
> His own lizard toes, never human hands. Keep exactly this look in every picture.
