# Gemini brief: the three pet buddies in ONE art style (teacher, 23 Nov 2026)

Decision: Pip's soft painted style, with a slightly thicker outline than Pip has now (between Pip's and Basil's), for all three.
Attach the reference pictures each time (see "How to use").

## How to use
1. Start a new Gemini chat and paste the **STYLE BLOCK** once.
2. Attach the references: `pip_sit_closed.png` (the style) and the buddy's current coloured picture (the colours and model),
   e.g. `momo_c_talk_closed.jpg` or `basil_c_sit_closed.jpg`.
3. Then ask for ONE picture at a time with the **PICTURE LINE** for that picture (list below).
4. For a talking or blink set, ask for the first picture, then say: *"Same picture exactly. Change ONLY the mouth to …"* so the body does not move.

## STYLE BLOCK (paste once)

> Draw a cute 2D children's cartoon character for an educational science video for 9-year-olds.
> Style: soft painted digital illustration, like a premium children's storybook. Gentle soft shading and soft, fluffy painted fur
> (or soft painted scales for the lizard). A clean, smooth, medium-thick dark navy outline (#1F2A44) around the whole character and
> its main shapes: a little bolder than a thin sketch line, but not a thick sticker outline. The same line thickness everywhere.
> Big, round, glossy dark eyes with a white highlight. Soft pink blush on the cheeks. Friendly, warm and expressive face.
> Not 3D, not a photo, not watercolour, not flat vector, no texture noise.
> Composition: ONE character only, full body, sitting, centred, filling about 85% of the image height, with a small white margin on
> every side. Plain pure white background. No ground shadow, no text, no labels, no frame, no props unless I ask.
> Image: square, 1024 x 1024.
> Match the reference picture's style (the grey chinchilla) exactly; keep the character's own colours and body from its own reference.
