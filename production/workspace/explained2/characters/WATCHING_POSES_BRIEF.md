# Watching poses: the painting brief (6 pictures)

**The teacher's request (2026-11-25):** "The pet buddies should look towards the magnet during this close up demonstration, not the audience." The teacher chose new paintings.

**How they are used:** in every close-up demonstration, the buddies watch the object. The engine flips each picture so the buddy faces whatever is being shown. Now and then, one buddy glances at the viewer and gives a small wave, then turns back to watch ("like a student not paying attention"); never during the key teaching moment. That wave uses the existing waving pose.

## Rules for every picture (the same as the colour painting list)
1. **Size and format:** 1024 × 1024 px, PNG or high-quality JPG, under 10 MB.
2. **Background:** plain white, with no ground shadow, no text, and no other buddy.
3. **Body:** full body, sitting on the ground, filling 80–90% of the height, with a small white margin on every side.
4. **The same model and colours as the current paintings:**
   - Pip: grey.
   - Momo: coral #FB7158, with tabby stripes and a cream belly.
   - Basil: teal, with a bright teal beard and tail bands.
   - The same outline colour and thickness, the same soft paint style, a pink blush and pink inner ears.
5. **The pose:** the same sitting body as the current sitting pose, but the **head turned to the viewer's LEFT, about three-quarters**, and the **eyes looking to the left and a little down**, as if watching something interesting on a table beside them. Front paws resting on the belly. Tail curled on the viewer's right, so it doesn't cover the face.
6. **Copy for the second picture:** paint the first picture of each pair, then copy it and change only the mouth.
7. **Sending:** one picture per message, named as below.
