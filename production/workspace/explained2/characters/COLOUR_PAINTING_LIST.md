## Rules for every picture

1. **Size:** 1024 × 1024 px (square), JPG (high quality) or PNG, under 10 MB.
2. **Background:** plain white. **No ground shadow**, no text, no marks, no other buddy.
3. **Full body**, sitting on the ground (except where a line says otherwise). The buddy fills **80–90% of the height**, with a small white margin on every side and nothing touching the edge.
4. **Same model as the grey paintings:** the same body shape, head size, eyes and proportions. Only the colours change.
5. **Outline:** the same thickness on all 33 pictures, closed all the way round with no gaps. Pick ONE outline colour for all three buddies (your coral Momo test uses a warm reddish-brown; that is fine if all three use it).
6. **Paint style:** the same softness on all 33. Your coral Momo test is the reference.
7. **Every buddy has:** soft pink blush on both cheeks, pink inner ears, and dark eyes with a white highlight.
8. **Never** green, blue or sun-yellow on a buddy (those colours mean answers and "YOU").
9. **Hands and paws:** a buddy has its own paws, not human hands.
10. **Names:** use the file name in each line, e.g. `momo_sit_closed.jpg`. Send **one picture per message**.
