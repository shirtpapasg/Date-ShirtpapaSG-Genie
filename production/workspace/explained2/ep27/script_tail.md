## 9. Teacher sheet (one screen)
- Ask before playing: "It's 33 °C. How would YOU keep ice cream frozen on a 10-minute walk?"
- Pause at the fix cards (0:24): children vote with fingers. Pause after the replay: "What did the fan really push?"
- After the journey: "Is wool warm, or is it slow?" Then the fair test: name what was changed, kept the same and measured.
- Exam words: "Heat flows from a hotter place to a colder place." "Wool is a poor conductor of heat; it slows down the heat flowing from
  the warmer surroundings to the ice cream."
- Try it at home (with an adult): two ice cubes on two saucers, one wrapped in a wool sock, one not; check after 15 minutes.
