# Pronunciation list (all episodes)

Heart and the buddies speak through Kokoro, which guesses from spelling. Words on this list are spoken from the exact sounds the
teacher approved. In code: `sg_pron.py`: `if needs_fix(text): audio, sr = speak_line(kokoro, text, voice, speed)`.

| Word | Say it | Phonemes | Approved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sungei Buloh | "Soon-ai, bur-LOW", with "low" gliding down about 5 semitones (short pause after "Soon-ai") | sˈuːnaɪ, bˈɜːɹloʊ + fall 5 | teacher, 2026-11-20 (clip H) |

Before adding a new Singapore name, make a test clip of Heart saying it in a sentence, send it to the teacher, and add it here
only once approved.
