  10. Honeybees: experiments suggest they can sense the Earth's magnetic field (about 80% sure; spoken as "Scientists think").
  11. Water striders stand on water: water-repellent hairs on their legs and the water's surface hold them up (very sure); common on Singapore's park ponds (very sure).
