# TheOneApp, Explained (revamped): the series format and rules

The teacher's rulebook for every new "TheOneApp, Explained" episode, P3 to P6, from 20 Nov 2026.
P3 EP1 ("We Put 6 Things in a Box for 30 Days. Which Ones Are ALIVE?") is the reference episode.
Its files are in `ep01/`; its notes rounds are in `ep01/NOTES_ROUND3.md`.

Also read:
- `STYLE_GUIDE.md`: the approved look.
- `BRIEF.md`: background and decisions.
- `success_examples.md`: the teacher's 8 model videos.
- `production/THEONEAPP_EXPLORED.md`: the sister series' rules 1 to 10.

---

## 1. What the series is

- One textbook topic per episode, told as a game the child plays at home or in class.
- The child is a player:
  - locks in an answer with their hands,
  - keeps score on their other hand,
  - plays "You vs the Buddies".
- Each episode has one story spine taken from the success examples. Pick ONE per episode, never three:
  - a "30-day box" (Dr. Plants),
  - a field trip (Sesame Street),
  - a build-and-reveal (Aquarium Info).
- **BE THE THING: the first-person journey into the micro-world (the teacher, 20 Nov: "I like this video's way of taking
  perspective of the particle / energy / material, moving into the microworld. This concept is new and I did not see it anywhere.")**
  Reference: TheOneApp, Explored EP9, "Mosquitoes VS Mosquitoes!" (https://youtu.be/F_oNWbfqtC0), plus Explored EP1 (a smoke
  particle), EP4 (a water molecule), EP5 (light energy), EP7 (oxygen) and EP10 (a nutrient).
  - The camera IS the particle, the energy or the material: the viewer becomes the water molecule, the bit of food, the oxygen,
    the light, the seed, the current. "You are a drop of water. You are in the wet soil…" Second person, present tense.
  - The world rushes past at that tiny scale: near things big and fast, far things small and slow; the familiar place (the window
    ledge, the canteen) seen from inside or below; a small "speeded up" or scale tag when time or size is changed.
  - It follows a real path through the science, step by step (soil → root → stem → leaf), with simple true words and a short card
    at each stop; then the camera pulls back out to normal size and the textbook words name what the viewer just lived through.
  - Every episode has at least one such journey (it is the series' signature, not an extra). Typically 20 to 40 s; it may be the
    spine of a part. Calm music, Heart's voice soft, room to breathe (the quiet-wonder look of Journey to the Microcosmos), so the
    loud game beats around it land harder.
  - True at the level taught: no P5/P6 labels in P3 (no "cells", "chloroplast" in P3). Nothing may teach a wrong idea.
- Real Singapore places always: HDB flat, void deck, community garden, canteen, MRT, Kranji farm.
- Exam-exact: every definition and key word is quoted word for word from the textbook (the P3 to P6 plan's `definitions` files).
  - Every non-textbook sentence goes on an approval list for the teacher before rendering.
- Nothing may teach a wrong idea:
  - Wrong ideas are only ever a buddy's guess.
  - They are corrected within seconds.
  - Heart never says them.
- Animals are always shown cared for: food, water, air holes, returned home.

## 2. Length and parts

- **Each video is about 3 to 5 minutes.** A topic that needs more is split into **Part 1 and Part 2**.
- Choose the split point so each part ends on a question or cliffhanger and each part has its own goals.
- **Part 1** ends with:
  - a tease of Part 2 ("Find out in Part 2!"),
  - its own score,
  - its own key-word recap.
- **Part 2** opens with:
  - a short silent recap montage,
  - then a "Last time…" line that is TRUE to what Part 1 showed.
- Each part keeps its own score ("You vs the Buddies").

## 3. Pacing (the teacher: "too fast… no time for students to digest")

**Slowed a further 5% (teacher, 2026-11-20, from EP6 on): "consider slowing down the pacing of the video by another 5%, especially areas where new terms are introduced."**
- **Heart:**
  - Kokoro af_heart at about speed 0.90 (was 0.95).
  - About 0.5 s between sentences (was 0.45 s).
- **Buddies:**
  - Each speaks in their Character Voice Kit voice.
  - About 12% slower than default (was 7%).
- **New key word:** when a key word is said for the first time, it pops LARGE with a chime and holds about 2.0 s (was 1.5 s), then
  flies to the key-word shelf. Heart pauses about 0.6 s after saying a new term before the next sentence, and the new term is said
  slowly and clearly (a little slower than the rest of the line).
- **Mark Sentence cards:** hold about 2.5 s of stillness after Heart finishes reading (was 2 s).
- **Definitions** (e.g. "similarities: characteristics that things have in common"):
  - on a light, readable strip (dark text on cream),
  - read aloud,
  - on screen about 3.5 s.
- **Length:** the slower pace makes parts longer. Keep each part at most about 5:15; if it runs over, cut non-teaching lines (game
  chatter, extra examples) first, never the pauses around new terms.
- Something changes on screen every 10 to 15 s.
- Do not trim teaching to hit a length. Split into parts instead.

## 4. Interaction rules (the viewer must have time to react)

- **"Say it with me / us!" is always SPOKEN, never just a bubble.**
  - The buddy next to the card (or all three) says it aloud with lip-sync and an inviting gesture.
  - Then a **0.8 s pause**.
  - Then Heart reads, so children say it together.
  - This applies to every Mark Sentence card and every chant.
- **The chant is call-and-response:**
  1. A buddy or all three say "Say it with us!", then pause 0.8 s.
  2. The buddies chant one line on the clap-clap-stomp beat.
  3. A **"YOUR TURN!"** tag pops by the YOU hands.
  4. A pause of about **1.5 s** lets the class repeat the line, with the beat continuing softly.
  5. Repeat for every line, then optionally once all together.
- **Lock-ins:**
  - A closed question with 2 or 3 choices.
  - Answered with the body: point left or right, or fingers 1, 2, 3.
  - A ring timer of 5 to 6 s, ticking that speeds up, a bell, then "LOCKED IN!".
  - The buddies reveal AFTER the viewer, each with a one-line reason.
  - The wrong idea rotates between buddies.
  - A buddy can be right for the wrong reason and gets no point.
  - LIVING = green, NON-LIVING = blue (or the topic's two choices in two fixed colours).
- **"HOLD ON!"** (not "FOUL!"): a buddy uses a bad rule or an unfair test, and the viewer crosses their arms.
- **Showdown:** a buddy's homework with one mistake to spot, for double points.
- **Score hand:** one finger per point. A perfect game ends in a high five.
- **The hidden lizard** (a cute little house lizard, never "cicak"):
  - Hide it somewhere in EVERY part.
  - ALWAYS reveal it before the part ends: "Did you spot the lizard? … Tell someone at home."
- **Key-word read-along before the end of every part:**
  1. The shelf moves to the centre and grows.
  2. Heart: "Let's read today's key words together! Ready?"
  3. Each word lights up as Heart says it, with about 1.2 s for the class to say it. The buddies mouth along.
  4. Heart: "Well done!"
- **The class says "Now you know!" at the very end of every part:**
  - The count-in "One!", "two!", "Now you know!" is evenly spaced: each starts exactly 1.0 s after the last.
  - "Now you know!" ends with a happy BLAST, never robotic: Heart's brightest take, pitched up and rising on "KNOW!", the buddies shouting in unison as a happy crowd, and a big sting on "know!" (brass hit, cymbal, confetti pop, "yay!"), with the title slam and a confetti burst on the same frame.
  1. "Say it with us!", then a 0.8 s pause.
  2. "Ready? One, two…"
  3. Heart and all three buddies shout "Now you know!" together, excited, with the title slamming in and the YOU hands joining.
- **The outro runs about 4 more seconds with the animation still going:**
  - The sunburst turns, the buddies bounce, the music fades.
  - Nothing new is spoken.
  - No text in the bottom fifth of the frame, where YouTube's overlays sit.

## 5. The cast

- **Heart:** host and narrator, voice only. Warm and fair, never wrong.
- **Pip (chinchilla), Basil (lizard), Momo (cat):** the teacher's own painted buddies, the same pets as in TheOneApp.
  - Pip is careful, Basil is bold, Momo is curious.
  - **Basil speaks more (teacher, 2026-11-20: "do give basil more speaking time").** He is the children's favourite voice: give him
    at least as many lines as Pip and Momo in every part, not only the wrong ideas. He leads some Mark Sentence cards and
    "Say it with me!" moments, explains things he gets right, cracks the jokes, and reacts on screen (his bold guesses are right
    about half the time).
  - They are good sports, and nobody is ever made to feel silly.
- **Art:**
  - The buddies are the teacher's own paintings, animated as cut-out sprites: `characters/sprites/` and `characters/sprites/poses/`.
  - No 3D. No code-drawn redraws of the buddies.
  - Fur stays natural, never recoloured.
- **Signature colours** (the same in TheOneApp, free and credit buddies):
  - Pip: purple #8B5CF6.
  - Basil: teal #14B8A6.
  - Momo: coral #FB7158.
  - No buddy may be green or blue (those are the answer colours) or sun-yellow #FFC83A (that is "YOU").
  - The colours are used on each buddy's aura disc, speech-bubble border and tail, name tag and pick card.
  - Bubble fill stays cream.
- **Buddies always sit in their circular aura disc** when shown next to real props (pots, boxes, plants, benches). They are presenters, not tiny animals in the set, so scale never looks wrong.
- **New poses:**
  - Make each from the buddy's own picture, one buddy per image, 1024 px, white background, no shadow.
  - Make talking/blink states by editing only the face.
  - Keep each buddy one consistent model.

## 6. Motion (reference: `characters/sprites/pose_test_robot_round.mp4`, approved "exactly what I want")

- **Pose swaps:**
  - 3 frames of squash,
  - a hard cut to the new pose stretched tall,
  - overshoot, a small hop, sparkles and dust.
  - NEVER cross-fade.
- **Idle life:** breathing, a slight sway, leaning towards the action, blinks.
- **Talking:**
  - lip-sync on the talking sprites;
  - a small drawn mouth plus a syllable bounce on one-face poses.
- **Objects never float or fly.**
  - Things travel low along the ground in small hops with dust puffs, or are carried or slid by the buddies.
  - Nothing hangs above the buddies' heads.
  - Objects keep their normal size.
- **Props must read clearly as what they are.** E.g. the experiment box is a clear glass box with a lid, air holes and an "OUR BOX" label, on the ground.
- **Every label and small print fits fully inside the frame.** Label lines never cross other objects.
- Frames are a pure function of t. Render in 10 s chunks so restarts lose little.

- **Say names right.** Singapore names Kokoro gets wrong are spoken from approved sounds: see `PRONUNCIATION.md` and `sg_pron.py`
  (e.g. Sungei Buloh = "Soon-ai, bur-low"). A new name gets a test clip for the teacher before it is used.
- **Real animals must look real (teacher, 2026-11-20).** Draw an animal's true shape from real photos before drawing it, then simplify
  into the flat cartoon style; never invent anatomy. Example: the monitor lizard from behind has a long narrow head on a long thick
  neck, a heavy body, short legs bent out at elbow and knee with claws down (never straight out like arms), a very long tapering
  tail, and very fine bead-like scales (never big hexagon tiles). Use the Singapore animal children really see: the Malayan water
  monitor (dark grey-brown, rows of pale yellow spots, paddle-shaped tail). Reference photos: `refs/` (gitignored, not for reuse).

## 7. On screen

- Series logo pill top left; the YOU vs BUDDIES score pill top right.
- **The key-word shelf, top right, under the score pill:**
  - Sun-yellow pills stack downwards, 3 per part where possible.
  - It slides aside during the shrink, full-screen cards, the cold open and the countdown.
  - It becomes the read-along at the end of the part.
- **Mark Sentence card:**
  - cream, with "MARK SENTENCE" and an "n of N" tag;
  - highlighter on the key words;
  - a footer with the level, theme and topic.
- No burned-in captions. Make an `.srt` for YouTube instead.
- Vocabulary:
  - use plain English for food names ("bean sprouts", not "taugeh");
  - say "lizard", not "cicak".

## 8. Thumbnails (reference: `ep01/thumb_ep01.jpg`, `ep01/thumb_part1.jpg`)

- Use a bright frame from the episode itself.
- Title words go on the BRIGHT side, right-aligned:
  - white first line(s), then sun-yellow line(s),
  - a thick dark-violet stroke.
- Keep the other side clear: no dark gradient and no extra logo pill. The frame's own small logo stays.
- For two-part episodes, add a tilted raspberry "PART 1" / "PART 2" pill with a white rim on the LEFT, bottom-left.
- Words must not cover any buddy's face.

## 9. Sound

- Heart's voice on top.
- A light bouncy bed, ducked under speech.
- A calm pad for the shrink.
- Effects: a woodblock tick that speeds up for lock-ins, a bell, a "LOCKED IN!" stamp, pops on pose swaps, the clap-clap-stomp chant beat, and a soft chime on new key words.
- Loudness about -16 LUFS.
- A human must listen before upload: the agents can only measure the sound.

## 10. Process

1. **Script first**, written as segment tables (time, on screen, exact words, sound, why), plus:
   - a list of the non-textbook sentences;
   - 3 Shorts;
   - a teacher sheet.
   The teacher approves before anything is rendered.
2. **Then build and render.** Check stills around every swap, card and mouth change, and send contact sheets.
3. **The teacher watches.** Their notes go in a NOTES file and are fixed in one re-render.
4. **Upload** to TheOneApp's YouTube:
   - Made for Kids, public, in the level playlist;
   - only when the teacher says so.
**Autopilot (teacher, 2026-11-20: "Upload into YouTube and make it live automatically. I trust the system.")** From P3 EP4 on, and
for finishing EP2: steps 1 and 3 no longer wait for the teacher. Write the script to this rulebook, build it, run the checks, then
upload it live with `production/youtube/explained_upload.py <epNN/upload.json>` (Made for Kids, public, thumbnails, playlists in
order). Send the teacher the finished parts and the list of things to check; their notes are fixed in a re-render and re-upload.
5. **Never delete an old video** that a new episode replaces (teacher, 2026-11-20). Its views count towards the channel and
   towards monetising it. Keep the old one up, take it out of the main level playlist, and point it to the new episode
   (a card, the end screen or a line in the description). Ask the teacher before making it unlisted or private:
   watch time on unlisted or private videos does not count towards YouTube's public watch hours.

## 11. Episode template (one part, about 4 to 5 min)

1. Silent cold open (a real Singapore place).
2. Hook and practice lock-in.
3. Rules and goals (the "Today you'll learn" chips).
4. "Are you ready? Three! Two! One! Go!"
5. Two rounds of lock-ins, each with its evidence and its Mark Sentence. A "Say it with me!" before each card.
6. The first-person "be the thing" journey into the micro-world (the series signature; see section 1).
7. "HOLD ON!" and/or the Showdown.
8. The chant as call-and-response.
9. The reveal or payoff.
10. The score hand.
11. The lizard reveal.
12. The key-word read-along.
13. Next episode's dare, or the Part 2 tease.
14. "Say it with us!", then the class shouts "Now you know!".
15. About 4 s of animated outro.
