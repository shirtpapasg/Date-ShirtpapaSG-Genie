# Primary 5 Science, Explained — Series Plan ("TheOneApp, Explained - <topic>")

**Source:** Inspiring Science Primary 5 (Standard), MOE CPDD / Marshall Cavendish, 2025 (Primary Science Syllabus 2023). **The P5 textbook scans on Drive hold no readable text**, so this plan is built from: the P5 Activity Book (text of Chapters 1–2 and the full table of contents, `sources/activity_book_p5_text.txt`), the syllabus learning outcomes and exclusions (`theoneapp/syllabus-p3p5.js`), the MOE 2025 monograph (theme takeaways), the teacher's own P5 recitation tables and WA1 trigger bank (Drive), and TheOneApp's 40 P5 School Lab benches and five P5 Mind Lab posters. Topic map: `content_map_p5.md`. Exact wording: `definitions_p5.md`. App links: `app_p5.md`, `labs_p5.json`. **Before release, the teacher checks every line flagged "textbook unverified" against the textbook.**

**How sources are used:** only as a map of topics, concepts, key terms and common mistakes. Every script is original writing. Nothing is copied apart from short technical terms and the exact wording of official definitions and the teacher's own exam lines. No textbook sentences, questions, cast names or pictures are reused.

**Audience:**
- Singapore P5 pupils (ages 10–11, Standard Science; Foundation pupils can watch too, the syllabus content is the same) and the parents helping them.
- US Grade 5 pupils (Grade 4–6 for the circuits and body-systems episodes).
- One video for both; each script carries one spoken **Singapore anchor** and one spoken **US bridge**.

**Format:** the finished P4/P3 design, built with the P3 kit plus the P5 `photo()` helper. 3:30–4:45, about 480–640 authored words. Hook → welcome with three goals → body beats → Try-it → Pause and predict (four options, 4-second "Think!" countdown) → Exam corner (≤ 3 full lines) → atomic habit → mind map on the TheOneApp phone → one offline ask → goodbye. Style rules: `01_style_guide_p5.md`. One brief per episode: `briefs/epNN.md`.

**Narrator:** Heart, voice 3 (expressive), every episode.

**Real photos (new):** photos for the real thing, drawings for the mechanism (style guide §11). Each brief carries a photo shot list with a fallback drawing, so a missing photo never blocks a render. Preferred source: the teacher's own photos in the Drive folder "P5 video photos/EPNN"; otherwise public-domain or CC-licensed photos with credits on screen and in the description.

## Why 27 episodes

The P5 Activity Book has 38 activities in six chapters (plus two thematic activities). P4 ran 22 episodes for a 159-page book; P3 ran 25. P5 pupils can take a little more per episode, but the chapters carry more distinct exam ideas (four separate processes in flowering-plant reproduction, five change-of-state processes, three evaporation factors, three gaseous-exchange cases, series and parallel each with their own rules), so the series runs to **27 episodes**, each with one big idea and exactly three goals. Every activity of the book lands in exactly one episode (coverage check at the end).

## What every brief contains (`briefs/epNN.md`)

4. Key words for the ⭐ shelf (segment, cue word, label, colour), full names.
5. Misconceptions to bust, written as "Exam tip" beats in positive language (the trigger bank is the main source).

7. Pause and predict: the question, **four options**, the answer and the reason.
8. The atomic habit for `habit1` and the mind map for `habit2` (middle word, branches, sub-branches matching the Mind Lab poster), ending with the real app steps and the chapter to pick.

11. Realism notes.
