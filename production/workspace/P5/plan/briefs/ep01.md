## Big idea
All living things are made up of cells; a cell is the basic unit of life, seen as a repeating pattern under a microscope at higher magnification.

## Misconceptions to bust
- A microscope makes the object grow bigger → the object stays the same size; it appears more enlarged, more so at a higher than at a lower magnification (row 1.4). → the `tip` beat.
- Higher magnification shows less of the detail → an object appears more enlarged at a higher magnification (row 1.4; AB 1.1 table: naked eye, lower, higher).
- Only animals (or only people) are made of cells → all living things are made up of cells, plants too (row 1.3; AB 1.1 samples: human skin, waterweed leaf, onion root). ⚠ check: standard P5 wording, not printed as a misconception in any source.
- Everything, even a grain of salt or a glass bead, is made of cells → cells are what living things are made of; salt, plastic and glass are not living things (built from row 1.3, ⚠ check). → wrong quiz options A, B and D.

## Pause and predict
- **Q:** A girl looks at four things under a microscope at a higher magnification. In which one will she see a pattern of cells?
- **A** a grain of salt · **B** a piece of plastic wrap · **C** a thin slice of cucumber · **D** a glass bead
- **Answer: C.** The cucumber is part of a living plant, and all living things are made up of cells; salt, plastic and glass are not living things.
- Checks: a new situation (cucumber was never shown); the question does not contain "made up of cells" or "basic unit"; the beat before (`tip`) is about magnification, not about which things have cells; A, B and D carry the misconception "everything is made of cells". ## Realism notes
- The microscope is a light microscope with an eyepiece, objective lenses, a stage with a glass slide and a light underneath; the slide sits on the stage, not floating.
- Cells are drawn only as a repeating pattern of plain outlines (plant cells boxy, skin cells flatter and less regular); **no organelles, no nucleus, no labels inside a cell** (style guide §7). Real photos will show dots inside cells: label nothing inside them.
- The naked-eye view of skin shows lines only; the lower-magnification view shows more detail but no clear cells; only the higher view shows the pattern (AB 1.1 table order).
- The waterweed leaf is a thin green water-plant leaf; the onion root is a thin white root tip, not the bulb.
- Magnifying glass: the object under it looks bigger but no cells appear; the lens is never pointed at the Sun.
- The redwood is tall with reddish bark; the kangkong leaf is arrow-shaped on a hollow stem.
- No numbered markers; labels on plain lines with no arrowheads; the dial reads "lower" and "higher", not numbers.
