## Big idea
In a series circuit there is one path; more batteries in series make the bulb brighter, more bulbs in series make each bulb dimmer, and when one bulb is removed all go out.

## Misconceptions to bust
- "Extra batteries only make the bulb last longer, not shine brighter." → The more batteries joined in series, the brighter the bulb (lab 6.1 Challenge mix-up). **Becomes the `tip` beat**, together with the next line.
- "The bulb is brighter" written with no comparison. → Always say brighter or dimmer than what (content_map Ch 6 common mistakes "'brighter' said without a comparison"). Shown in the `tip` beat as the ✓ line (not marked ✗: the short sentence is incomplete, not false).
- "Removing any bulb switches everything off." → Only a gap switches the bulbs off; with the gap joined, the loop is closed again (content_map Ch 6 common mistakes). **Wrong quiz option C.**
- "Every bulb still gets its own full share." → In one series loop each bulb is dimmer as bulbs are added (lab 6.2 prediction reason). **Wrong quiz option D.**
- "More bulbs make the circuit stronger, so every bulb is brighter." → The more bulbs joined in series, the dimmer each bulb (lab 6.2 trap; content_map Ch 6).
- "Batteries work whichever way round." → They must be joined + to −; one turned round makes the bulb dimmer or it does not light up (content_map Ch 6; 6.4).
- Avoid the lab 6.1 reason wording "make electricity flow harder" (app_p5.md script check): say "the bulb is brighter".

## Pause and predict
**Q:** Three bulbs glow in one series loop. One bulb is taken out and its two wires are joined together, so the loop is complete again. What happens to the two bulbs left?
- A. Each is brighter than before.
- B. Each is dimmer than before.
- C. Both go out. (misconception: removing any bulb switches everything off)
- D. Each stays exactly as bright. (lab 6.2 reason: "Every bulb still gets its own full share.")

**Answer: A.** Reason: the loop is complete, so electricity still flows, and with fewer bulbs in the series loop each bulb is brighter than with three.
Rule check: a new situation (the `out` beat leaves a gap; here the gap is joined, and the change goes from three bulbs down to two, the reverse of the `bulbs` beat); the stem does not say brighter, dimmer or "the more bulbs"; the `tip` beat before `quiz1` is about extra batteries and writing the comparison, not about bulbs or taking a bulb away.
