## 2. Big idea
All living things need water for their life processes; water must be conserved and kept clean (litter pollutes reservoirs); a solar still uses evaporation and condensation to get cleaner water from dirty water, but it may still hold bacteria.

## 7. Misconceptions to bust
- "The cling wrap filters the mud / the pebble cleans the water" → only the water evaporates; the mud does not turn into a gas and stays behind (bench 2.8 written-answer do-not-accept list; bench 2.8 Challenge mix-up "It cleans the water by trapping the mud"). **This is the `tip` beat.**
- "The water caught is clean, so it is safe to drink" → it may look cleaner but may hold bacteria and may not be safe to drink (AB 2.8 safety note; app_p5.md script check: the bench calls it "clean"). Busted in `safe`.
- "The same amount of water gives the same amount caught" → a larger exposed surface area evaporates faster (Trigger bank p. 3). **Wrong quiz option C.**
- "Water must boil before it can turn into water vapour" (bench 2.4 tagged mix-up question) → **wrong quiz option D**.
- "Condensation only happens when it is cold, so a cool spot catches more" (bench 2.8 prediction reason) → description note.
- "A running tap only wastes a tiny bit of water" (bench 2.6 tagged mix-up question) → the bench's 8 litres vs 2 litres in `save`.
- "A little litter cannot affect the water" (bench 2.7 tagged mix-up question) → description note.

## 8. Exam wording box
1. All living things need water to carry out life processes for survival.
2. Human activities such as littering can pollute our water bodies.
3. Sea water gains heat and evaporates into water vapour. The water vapour touches a cooler surface, loses heat and condenses into water droplets. The droplets flow down and are collected. (cause → process → result)
