## Misconceptions to bust
- Living things reproduce to stay alive longer, or to keep the parents alive → they reproduce to ensure the continuity of their own kind (row 1.7). → the `tip` beat.
- Only animals reproduce → plants reproduce to continue their species too (row 1.6). → the `tip` beat.
- Deer (animals) with food and space always increase in number → without young, numbers fall as older members are gone (lab 1.2 prediction reason, `labs_p5.json`). → wrong quiz options A and D.
- Numbers only fall when people take some animals out → without reproduction, the number falls even when nobody takes any away (lab 1.2 Challenge, tagged "The trap"). → wrong quiz option B.
- A graph's trend can be read from one point → read the line from left to right and give the direction (AB 1.2 Part B step 3a).

## Pause and predict
- **Q:** On a small island, a kind of bird has plenty of food and space. But for many years, none of its eggs hatch. What happens to the number of these birds?
- **A** it grows, because they have food and space · **B** it stays the same, because nobody takes any birds away · **C** it falls, because no young replace the older birds · **D** it grows, because the adults never grow old
- **Answer: C.** No young hatch, so nothing replaces the older birds as they are gone; only reproduction ensures the continuity of their kind.
