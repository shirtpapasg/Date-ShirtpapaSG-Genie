## 7. Misconceptions to bust
- "It dries faster because it is bigger / it dries" (vague words) → write "larger exposed surface area" and "the rate of evaporation increases" or "evaporates faster" (Mix-up buster #15: "FASTER", "exposed SURFACE AREA"). **This is the `tip` beat.**
- "A test that changes two things at once still tells you the answer" → only the variable being tested may change; the others are kept the same (CfU Q5; bench 2.4 Challenge "What must stay the same?"). **Wrong quiz options B, C and D** (each pair changes the wrong thing or more than one thing).
- "The folded towel takes up less space, so it dries faster" → folding hides water from the air: smaller exposed surface area, slower evaporation (bench 2.4 prediction reason).
- "The fan only cools the towel, so it dries more slowly" → moving air carries the water vapour away, so evaporation is faster (bench 2.4 prediction reason).
- "Water can only evaporate at its boiling point" → the towels lose water at room temperature (bench 2.4 "The trap"; EP 12).
- Humidity is NOT taught (content map flag 2: AB 2.4 and the teacher name only three factors).

## 9. Pause and predict
- **Question:** You want to test whether wind speeds up evaporation. Look at the four set-ups in the table (P: fan on, 30 °C, 50 cm²; Q: fan off, 30 °C, 50 cm²; R: fan on, 25 °C, 50 cm²; S: fan off, 30 °C, 80 cm²). Which two make a fair test?
- **A** P and Q. · **B** P and R. · **C** Q and S. · **D** R and S.
- **Answer: A.**
- **Reason:** P and Q differ only in the fan; the temperature of the surroundings and the exposed surface area are kept the same, so any difference in mass comes from the wind. (B changes the temperature, not the wind; C changes the exposed surface area, not the wind; D changes all three.)
- Checks: a new situation modelled on CfU Q5 with new set-ups (CfU Q5's own answer is B and C for temperature); the `tip` beat before `quiz1` is about wording, not about choosing set-ups; the question does not contain the answer's words.
