## 2. Big idea
The water cycle is the continuous movement of water from the Earth to the sky and back; water in seas and lakes gains heat from the Sun and evaporates, water vapour rises, loses heat to the cooler air and condenses into tiny droplets that form clouds, and the droplets join, grow heavier and fall as rain.

## 7. Misconceptions to bust
- "The cold gets into the water vapour" / "the air gets cooler, so the vapour gets cold" → the water vapour loses heat to the cooler air (style guide §1; content map flag 5: lab brief 2.5's "the air gets cooler" is confusing). **This is the `tip` beat.**
- Naming no source of heat → water gains heat from the Sun (Mix-up buster #8: name the right source; bench 2.5 Challenge mix-up "The sea cools down at night, so more of its water turns into water vapour"). **`tip` beat.**
- "Clouds are water vapour" → clouds are tiny droplets of liquid water; water vapour is an invisible gas (Trigger bank p. 5; bench 2.5 do-not-accept "clouds are made of water vapour that we can see").
- "Clouds are made of smoke" (bench 2.5 prediction reason) → tiny water droplets.
- "Higher up is closer to the Sun, so it must be warmer" (bench 2.5 prediction reason) → the air higher up is cooler.
- "Water leaks through glass" (bench 2.3 mix-up) → **wrong quiz option D**; "living things make new water" → **wrong quiz option A**.
