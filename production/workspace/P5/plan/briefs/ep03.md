## Big idea
Characteristics are passed on from parents to their young during reproduction; a child inherits from both parents, so the combination differs.

## Misconceptions to bust
- Characteristics come from the mother only → characteristics are passed on from both parents (row 1.14; lab 1.3 prediction reason "Only the mother passes on characteristics"). → the `tip` beat.
- A child is a copy of one parent → the child inherits traits from both parents, so the combination of traits may be different (row 1.14).
- Only humans pass characteristics to their young → animals and plants do too (lab 1.3 prediction reason "Only humans pass characteristics to their young"). → the `spot` and `family` beats.
- Anything a child has is inherited (a name, a scar, a suntan) → only characteristics passed on during reproduction are inherited (lab 1.3 Challenge mix-ups "The name its owner gave it", "The bowl it eats from"). → wrong quiz options A, B and D.
- The counters (tokens) are genes → they are a model only; never call them genes (`labs_p5.json` 1.3 caution).

## Exam wording box
