## Big idea
Air is a mixture of gases; we breathe in air through the nose, down the windpipe into the lungs, where oxygen passes into the blood and carbon dioxide passes out; exhaled air has less oxygen, more carbon dioxide and more water vapour (limewater turns chalky).

## Misconceptions to bust
- We breathe in only oxygen and breathe out only carbon dioxide → both are air, a mixture of gases; exhaled air still has some oxygen, just less (misconception M-27; Mix-up buster #56; lab 4.2B Challenge; definitions 4.8). **Becomes the `tip` beat.**
- Water alone, not a gas, makes limewater cloudy → carbon dioxide turns limewater chalky (lab 4.2B Part (a) prediction reason). **Wrong quiz option B.**
- The air we breathe is mostly oxygen → most of the air is nitrogen (lab 4.1 Challenge). **Wrong quiz option C leans on it** (oxygen is not what turns limewater chalky).
- Air goes straight into the lungs through the chest → air passes through the nose and down the windpipe (lab 4.2A Challenge).
- The mouth is part of the respiratory system → the mouth belongs to the digestive system; air enters through the nose and the windpipe (Mix-up buster #55; ⚠ check against the textbook, which may mention the mouth: definitions 4.4).
- "Breathing tube" → write windpipe (Mix-up buster #59).
