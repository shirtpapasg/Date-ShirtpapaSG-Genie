## Big idea
The respiratory and circulatory systems work together: blood collects oxygen in the lungs and delivers it to the body parts, then returns carbon dioxide to the lungs; when we are active, the body parts need more oxygen, so the heart beats faster and we breathe faster; plants and humans both transport substances but in different ways.

## Misconceptions to bust
- The heart beats faster to make more blood → it beats faster to carry blood, with oxygen and digested food, to the muscles more quickly (content map Ch 4 common mistakes; definitions 4.21). **Becomes the `tip` beat.**
- The blood goes heart, body, lungs, heart (lungs skipped or reversed) / straight from the body to the lungs → body → heart → lungs → heart → body (lab 4.7 Part (a) and Challenge; definitions 4.20). **Wrong quiz option A.**
- Blood stays where it delivers oxygen → it keeps moving round the loop (lab 4.7 "Heart, lungs, body, and never back"). **Wrong quiz option D.**
- Resting gives the higher heart rate → the more vigorous the activity, the higher the heart rate (lab 4.6 Challenge; reading the 4.6 table).
- The heart beats faster to make up for slow lungs → breathing speeds up too (lab 4.6 Part (c)).
- Blood carries air → blood carries oxygen (lab 4.7 Challenge).
- Plants have blood vessels / plants carry digested food → plants have water-carrying and food-carrying tubes and no pump; plants make food (lab 4.5; definitions 4.24).
