# EP 21 — A pump and 100,000 kilometres of pipes: the circulatory system

## Big idea
The circulatory system is the heart, blood vessels and blood; the heart pumps blood around the body; blood carries oxygen, digested food and water to every part and carries carbon dioxide and waste substances away.

## Misconceptions to bust
- The lungs pump blood → the heart pumps blood; the lungs are where blood takes in oxygen and gives off carbon dioxide (misconception M-26). **Becomes the `tip` beat**, with Mix-up buster #59 (write "circulatory system", not "circulation system").
- Blood carries air → blood carries oxygen (and digested food, water, carbon dioxide and waste), not air (lab 4.4 Challenge; definitions 4.19). **Wrong quiz option A** leans on it (the windpipe carries air, not food).
- Food-carrying tubes in people → food-carrying tubes are plant tubes; humans carry digested food in the blood, through blood vessels (definitions 4.24; lab 4.5 "Plants also have blood vessels" mix-up, turned round). **Wrong quiz option C.**
- "The blood is transported around the body" as a full answer → the blood transports oxygen and digested food to all parts and carries carbon dioxide away (Mix-up buster #58).
- Blood is blue in some vessels → blood is always red; any blue on a diagram is only a code (lab 4.4 script check). The video draws every vessel red.

## Pause and predict
Q: A girl eats chicken rice at recess. Later that day, some digested food from her lunch reaches the muscles in her legs. How did it get there? A, along her windpipe. B, in her blood, through blood vessels. C, through food-carrying tubes. D, it soaked from her stomach straight through her body. **Answer: B.** Reason: the small intestine absorbs digested food into the blood, and the heart pumps the blood through blood vessels to all parts of the body, her leg muscles too; food-carrying tubes are in plants, not people. ## Realism notes
- Heart: drawn with two sides (left and right halves), about fist-sized relative to the body, in the chest between the two lungs, slightly to the left; pink/maroon muscle (style guide §10).
- Blood vessels: all red (this brief's choice, style guide §10); thick near the heart, branching into thinner ones that reach the fingertips and toes; one connected network, no loose ends.
- Blood moves only along the vessels, away from the heart and back to it; arrows only for this flow; label lines plain, no arrowheads; no numbered markers.
- Pulse: two fingers (not the thumb) on the thumb side of the inner wrist.
- Stethoscope: the chest piece on the chest, slightly left of centre; ear tips in the listener's ears (no faces).
- Cargo chips ride inside the vessel; "oxygen" is never drawn as air bubbles in the blood.
