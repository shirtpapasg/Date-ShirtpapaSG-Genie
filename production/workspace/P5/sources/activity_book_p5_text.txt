Preface
The Inspiring Science Activity Book complements the Textbook. In the Activity Book, students will explore Science concepts through hands-on activities that allow them to observe, manipulate apparatus and design different ways to understand Science around them.
Hands-on activities are designed to develop students’ cognitive, psychomotor and affective domains. These activities provide opportunities for students to understand the ways of thinking and doing Science.
Through hands-on activities, students will explore concepts in different contexts.
The series includes the following components:
• Inspiring Science Textbook (Primary)
