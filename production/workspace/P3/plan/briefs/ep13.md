# EP 13 — Design a school bag like an engineer

## Big idea
Engineers choose materials by their physical properties: decide what the object is for, list its parts, decide each part's use, draw the design and choose the materials (the book's five steps); after designing, engineers test their product, and keeping the great variety of living things matters.

## Misconceptions to bust
- "A new material always makes it better." It must suit the job, and a test must show the improvement (lab 3.8).
- "Pick the material first." First decide what the object is for.
- "Testing means trying once." Compare counts before and after; repeat.
- "Testing is step six of the design process." The book's process has five steps; testing is what engineers do after designing.

## Exam wording box
1. Knowing the physical properties of a material is important when designing and making new products.
2. It is important to choose a material whose physical property is suitable for the intended use of the object.
3. The materials are selected for the different functions of the parts based on their physical properties.
