## Big idea
Animals are grouped by observable characteristics, especially the outer covering: amphibians (moist skin, skin and lungs, eggs in water), birds (feathers, beak, wings, eggs) and fish (scales, fins, gills).

## Misconceptions to bust
- "It cannot fly, so it is not a bird." Feathers decide.
- "It swims and lays eggs, so it is a fish." Frogs, ducks and mudskippers show why the covering and breathing matter.
- "All fish lay eggs." Guppies and some sharks give birth to their young alive.
- "A starfish is a fish." No scales, no gills.
- "Every animal belongs to one of the six groups." Many animals (sea stars, spiders, worms, snails) belong to other groups.

## Exam wording box
1. Animals are living things that usually have parts such as a head, a body and limbs. Animals are unable to make their own food. They depend on other living things for food.
2. Amphibians are animals that can live both on land and in water.
3. Birds have an outer covering of feathers. Fish have an outer covering of scales.
