## Big idea
A food chain shows how energy is transferred from one organism to another; it always begins with a producer (a plant, which makes food by photosynthesis); animals are consumers; a predator hunts and eats another animal, the prey; overlapping food chains form a food web; the arrows point from the eaten to the eater.

## Misconceptions to bust
- The arrow points to what is eaten ("frog → water snail" read as "the frog eats") → the arrow points from the eaten to the eater and means "energy is transferred to" (style guide §4; lab BRIEF-P6 mix-up). **Hook and `chain`.**
- The Sun is the first organism / the producer in a food chain → the Sun gives light but is not an organism; the chain begins with the producer (style guide §4). **Becomes the `tip` beat.**
- All consumers are predators (TLG alternative conception, `definitions_p6.md` 4.15) → a consumer that eats plants is not a predator. **Becomes quiz option D.**
- Plants are prey → prey is "the animal that is eaten" (4.14). **Quiz option A.**
- A food web is a different thing from food chains → it is food chains in a community that are linked (4.17).
- Energy is used up along the chain → say it is transferred; never "used up" (style guide Never-list; `definitions_p6.md` 4.25).
