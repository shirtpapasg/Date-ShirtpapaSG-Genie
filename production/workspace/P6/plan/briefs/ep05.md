## Big idea
The energy in food is released during respiration, a process that needs oxygen and releases energy together with carbon dioxide and water; plants and animals respire all the time; photosynthesis happens only in plants and only with light, takes in carbon dioxide and gives out oxygen.

## Misconceptions to bust
- Plants photosynthesise in the day and respire only at night → plants respire all the time, day and night; they photosynthesise only when there is light (row 1.26; definitions alt-conception list "Plants photosynthesise but do not respire"). **This is the `tip` beat.**
- Plants rest at night and do nothing → they respire all night (rows 1.28, 1.29). **Quiz option C.**
- Plants take in carbon dioxide all the time → only during photosynthesis, only in light; in the dark they give out carbon dioxide (row 1.28). **Quiz options B and D.**
- Plants do not respire; only animals do → respiration occurs in both plants and animals (row 1.28).
- Respiration is breathing → respiration releases energy from food; breathing faster takes in the oxygen it needs (rows 1.25, 1.35). The narration never says "plants breathe" (style §1).
- Energy is used up → never said; energy is released from food (row 1.25).
- A plant in the dark makes more starch to store energy for later → without light no new food is made, and the stored starch decreases (lab 1.4 Challenge mix-up).

## Exam wording box
1. The energy needed by plants and animals for their life processes is released from food during a process called respiration.
2. This process requires oxygen and releases energy together with carbon dioxide and water.
3. Structured answer: Why does a leaf kept in the dark have less starch? In the dark, the plant cannot photosynthesise (cause), but it respires all the time (process), so its stored starch decreases (result).
