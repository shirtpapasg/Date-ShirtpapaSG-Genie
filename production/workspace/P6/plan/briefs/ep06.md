# EP 06 — Moving things have it: kinetic energy

## Big idea
Energy is required for things to work; kinetic energy is the energy a moving object has; it increases when the object's speed increases and when its mass increases.

## Misconceptions to bust
- "A heavy object always has kinetic energy." → Only a moving object has kinetic energy; a parked lorry has none (2.6, 2.9). **Becomes the `tip` beat.**
- "Only speed decides kinetic energy." → Kinetic energy depends on speed and mass (2.7, 2.8). **Becomes quiz option B.**
- "A heavier object has more kinetic energy" (with no condition). → Only when compared at the same speed (2.8 note). Taught in `mass`, `compare` and `marker`.
- "Kinetic energy is moving energy." → Use the term "kinetic energy" (house rule, 2.6).
- "Energy is the ability to do work." → Not the P6 book's wording; use 2.3 (recitation ⚠ check).

## Exam wording box
1. Kinetic energy is a form of energy that a moving object has.
2. The kinetic energy of an object depends on its speed and mass.
3. Structured answer (cause → process → result): The full bus has a greater mass than the empty bus. At the same speed, kinetic energy increases when mass increases, so the full bus has more kinetic energy.
