# EP 12 — A push or a pull: what a force is and what it can do

## Big idea
A force is a push or a pull; objects can exert a force in contact or without contact (magnets, a falling ball); a force can change the shape of an object, move a stationary object, speed up, slow down or stop a moving object, and change the direction of a moving object.

## Misconceptions to bust
- "Any change is a force (warming up, melting)." → A force is always a push or a pull; gaining heat is not a force (p. 49; Let's Apply Q1 p. 68, row 3.40). **Becomes the `tip` beat.**
- "A force only acts on still objects / a moving ball cannot be affected." → A force can speed up, slow down, stop or change the direction of a moving object (p. 52). **Becomes quiz option D.**
- "A force has only one effect at a time." → A force may produce one or more effects (p. 51). Taught in the quiz answer.
- "Forces need touching." → Some forces act without contact: magnets, a falling ball (p. 50). Shown in `contact`.
- "Push and pull are different things from force." → A push or a pull IS a force (p. 49).
