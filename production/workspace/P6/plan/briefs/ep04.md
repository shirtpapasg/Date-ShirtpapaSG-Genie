# EP 04 — Prove it with iodine: light, carbon dioxide and the bubble count

## Big idea
A leaf that photosynthesised turns iodine solution from brown to blue-black (starch); covering part of a leaf shows light is needed; more light (torch closer) or more carbon dioxide means more bubbles of oxygen, a faster rate of photosynthesis; van Helmont's willow showed the mass came from the air, not the soil.

⚠ check (big idea wording): the book says only that "plants absorb carbon dioxide from the air during photosynthesis" (row 1.23b); the narration says that and does not claim the whole mass came from the air.

## Misconceptions to bust
- The bubbles are carbon dioxide → the bubbles contain the gas produced during photosynthesis: oxygen (row 1.31; lab 1.3B Challenge mix-up). **This is the `tip` beat.**
- Every part of a leaf makes starch, or a leaf has starch just because it is green-shaped → only parts with chlorophyll that receive light carry out photosynthesis and store starch (rows 1.3, 1.24b). **Quiz options A and C.**
- A leaf in the dark still makes food → no light, no photosynthesis (row 1.33; lab 1.3B Challenge mix-up "a leaf in the dark still turns blue-black because it is still green"). **Quiz option D's opposite and the `marker` beat.**
- Plants take their food from the soil → van Helmont's soil kept the same mass; plants absorb carbon dioxide from the air during photosynthesis (row 1.23b).
- Moving the torch further away has no effect once it is lit → less light, fewer bubbles, a slower rate (lab 1.3B mix-up).
- Iodine solution turns blue-black on any leaf → only in the presence of starch (row 1.24b).

## Exam wording box
1. The colour of iodine solution changes from brown to blue-black in the presence of starch.
2. Describe the relationship: as the distance between the torch and the plant increases, the number of bubbles decreases.
3. Structured answer: Do you agree the bubble test works in a completely dark room? No. There is no light (cause), so the plant cannot carry out photosynthesis (process), and no oxygen bubbles are produced (result).
