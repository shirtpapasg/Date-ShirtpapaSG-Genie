## Big idea
The Sun is the primary source of energy: it gives the Earth heat and light; light is absorbed by plants during photosynthesis and stored as sugar; animals get the energy by eating plants or other animals; Singapore imports more than 90% of its food (30 by 30 goal).

## Misconceptions to bust
- Only plants depend on the Sun → animals depend on it too, indirectly, through the food they eat (rows 1.18, 1.19). **This is the `tip` beat.**
- The energy in food starts in the soil → plants use light energy from the Sun to make food (row 1.29; van Helmont in EP 04 shows the soil's mass stays the same). **Quiz option C.**
- The arrow means "eats", so it points from the eater to the eaten → each arrow points from the one that is eaten to the one that eats it and shows energy being transferred (row 1.17; style §4 food chains; lab 1.5 Part (a)). **Quiz option B.**
- An animal that eats only meat does not need the Sun → its energy comes from animals that ate plants (lab 1.2 check question). **The `marker` beat.**
- The Sun only keeps us warm → it gives heat and light; the light is what plants use to make food (row 1.14).

## Exam wording box
1. The Sun is the primary source of energy as living things get energy directly or indirectly from it.
2. Structured answer: Why does a fox depend on the Sun for energy? Grass uses light energy from the Sun to make food (cause), the rabbit eats the grass and the fox eats the rabbit (process), so the energy in the fox's food originates from the Sun (result).
