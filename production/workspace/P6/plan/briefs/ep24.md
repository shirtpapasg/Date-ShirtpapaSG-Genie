## Big idea
Conservation protects endangered plants and animals; reforestation restores forests; everyone can help: buy only what you need, reuse, recycle (Singapore's Zero Waste Nation bins), save energy and water; small human actions can have a positive or a negative impact (sea-turtle hatchlings and beach lights).

## Misconceptions to bust
- A partial conservation action is not worth doing unless it matches the full result → 3 → 9 hatchlings is a real positive impact (lab 5.3 planned mix-up). **Becomes the `tip` beat.**
- One person's action is too small to matter → "Everyone can play a part to save the Earth." (5.10). **Becomes quiz option C.**
- Light helps hatchlings see the way to the sea → artificial light draws them the wrong way (lab 5.3 Part (a) wrong reason).
- Litter gives hatchlings somewhere to hide, so more survive → litter blocks or slows their path (lab 5.3 Part (b) wrong reason).
- Recycling is the only way to help → buy only what you need and reuse come first (5.11).
- Interactions are only between living things → also with non-living things, and forces are interactions too (I0.1).
