## Big idea
Human actions change the environment: deforestation destroys habitats, so organisms lose food and shelter, and bare soil is washed away; pollution of air, water and land harms living things; burning fossil fuels releases carbon dioxide that traps heat, causing global warming and rising sea levels; natural resources are being depleted.

## Misconceptions to bust
- Carbon dioxide produces heat → it traps heat (Recitation Ch 6, 5.13). **Becomes the `tip` beat.**
- Cutting trees only harms the animals that lived in them → bare soil is washed away (5.16), soil reaches ponds and blocks light (5.17), and fewer trees take in carbon dioxide (5.14). **Quiz options C and D.**
- More light reaches a muddy pond, so plants grow more → soil blocks light. **Quiz option B.**
- Pollution stays where it is made → wind carries pollutants to other areas (5.23; the haze hook).
- A small amount of pollutant has no real impact → it still lowered the plants from 19.7 to 14.0 (lab 5.2 check question).
- A polluted population becomes extinct → it decreases (lab 5.2 planned mix-up).
