# EP 08 — Light, electrical, sound, heat: the other four forms

## Big idea
Light energy allows us to see (we see an object when light from it enters our eyes); electrical energy is present in a closed circuit and lets devices work; sound energy allows us to hear; heat energy is related to changes in temperature (gain heat energy, temperature may increase).

- "We see because our eyes give out light." → We see an object when light from it enters our eyes; the light is given off or reflected by the object (2.19). **Becomes quiz option A.**

- "Cold flows into the spoon / the spoon gets the soup's heat by magic." → The spoon gains heat energy from the hotter soup (house rule: gains heat from / loses heat to). Shown in `marker`.
