## Big idea
Energy can be converted from one form to another: the potential energy of the chemicals in a battery → electrical energy → light and heat energy in a bulb; a falling apple's potential energy → kinetic energy; a stretched band's potential energy → kinetic energy of the object it launches; a radio's electrical energy → sound energy.

## Misconceptions to bust
- "The switch gives the torch its energy." → The switch only closes the circuit; the energy comes from the potential energy of the chemicals in the battery (2.25; lab 2.2A planned mix-up "the fan's energy comes from the switch, not the cells"). **Becomes the `tip` beat.**
- "The chain can be written in any order." → Start from the form that goes in first; electrical → light is backwards for a solar panel (2.36). **Becomes quiz option B.**
- "The apple has kinetic energy while it hangs." → No kinetic energy because it is not moving (2.26). Shown in `apple`.
- "Energy is used up when the torch goes dim." → Energy is converted from one form to another (2.24; mix-up 33). Description note (taught fully in EP 10).
- "Write 'electricity' in a chain." → "electrical energy" (mix-up 18; EP 08 tip).
