## Why 24 episodes

The P6 book has five chapters and 26 Activity Book activities (1.1A–1.5, 2.1–2.4, 3.1–3.7, 4.1–4.4, 5.1–5.3). P6 pupils can carry more per episode than P5, but the PSLE marks explanations, so each episode keeps one big idea, three goals and one fully worked structured answer. Twenty-four episodes cover every section heading and every Let's Apply question once (coverage check at the end), with the four force types kept to one episode each because each is examined on its own.

## Release order suggestion

1. **Default: book order = school order.** P6 classes take Energy (photosynthesis, then energy conversion) before Interactions (forces, environment). The teacher's WA1 covers Energy from Food, Types of Energy, Forces and Living Together, which matches releasing 1 → 21 before it.
2. **PSLE timing.** The whole series should be out before the mid-year revision window; the forces and environment episodes double as revision for Standard and Foundation pupils (Foundation: skip EP 6–11 Energy Conversion and the elastic-spring part of EP 16, which the description says).
3. **School Lab gate.** Seven P6 benches are built (1.1A, 1.1B, 1.2, 1.3A, 1.3B, 1.4, 1.5), all in review; episodes 1 to 5 link them once signed off. The other episodes ship with `lab: null` and a home try-it until their bench is built; the brief's lab section names the planned bench so the card can be added later.
4. **Textbook gate for Chapters 4–5.** Every `definitions_p6.md` row flagged "textbook unverified" (EP 18–24) is checked by the teacher against the printed book before that episode goes public. The quick fix is to photograph pp. 74–110 and drop them in Drive.
5. **Groups that must stay adjacent:** 3 → 4 → 5 (photosynthesis, its tests, respiration), 6 → 7 → 8 → 9 (forms of energy, then conversion), 12 → 13 → 14 → 15 → 16 → 17 (forces), 18 → 19 → 20 → 21 (environment), 22 → 23 → 24.
