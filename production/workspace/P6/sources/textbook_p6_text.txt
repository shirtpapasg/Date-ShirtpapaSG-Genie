Kinetic energy is a form of energy that a moving object has. When an object is moving, we say that it has kinetic energy.
The toy car has kinetic energy when it is moving.

The kinetic energy of an object increases when its mass increases.
Travelling at the same speed, a bus full of passengers has more kinetic energy than the same bus without passengers. This is because it has a greater mass.
Rolling at the same speed, a heavier ball has more kinetic energy than a lighter ball.

Potential energy
Potential energy is a form of energy that an object has because of its position or state. It is also called stored energy. It is the energy that an object possesses because of its height, its state of being stretched or compressed, or the chemicals it contains.
Potential energy due to height
When an object is at a position above the ground, it is said to have potential energy. The potential energy that an object has due to its height above the ground is also known as gravitational potential energy.
The bird resting on the tree has potential energy because of its position above the ground.
The toy car placed at the top of the bookshelf has potential energy because of its position above the ground.
The pencil on the table has potential energy because of its position above the ground.

Some objects contain chemicals. These chemicals store energy that can be released when they interact with other chemicals. The energy stored in chemicals is called chemical potential energy.

Energy can be converted from one form to another.
When the switch in an electric circuit is closed, current flows in the circuit. The bulb in the circuit lights up and becomes hot.
The chemicals in a battery possess potential energy. When a battery is connected to a bulb in a closed circuit, the potential energy of the chemicals is converted to electrical energy. The electrical energy is then converted to light and heat energy when the bulb lights up.
When an apple is hanging on a tree, it has potential energy due to its height above the ground. The apple has no kinetic energy because it is not moving.
When the apple falls from the tree, it has kinetic energy because it is moving. As the apple falls, its potential energy decreases due to the decrease in height. We say that the potential energy of the apple is converted to kinetic energy as it falls.

56 Interactions
For a long time, people believed that an object would stop moving unless a force was continually exerted to move the object. However, this belief did not explain certain observations, such as why an object could continue moving even after the force exerted on it was removed. An example is an arrow moving even after being released from a bow.
To find out more about motion, Galileo Galilei carried out experiments using balls and slopes. Even though he did not have a clock, he devised ways to measure time in his experiments, such as using water clocks. His findings deepened our understanding of forces and motion, and were further developed by other scientists. This shows how scientific knowledge is continually refined over time.
Galileo’s experiments using balls and slopes
Understanding forces such as frictional force is important as it leads to designs that reduce frictional force during movement. For example, a racing car has a streamlined shape to reduce the frictional force between the car and air. This allows the car to move faster.
The streamlined shape of a racing car reduces the frictional force between the car and air.
Frictional force plays an important role in our everyday lives.
Frictional force keeps us from slipping when we walk on the ground. When an object moves on a smooth surface, the frictional force acting on the object is less than when the object moves on a rough surface. This is why we slip more easily when we walk on a smooth surface.
Frictional force allows us to hold objects. When we hold a glass of water with our hand, the frictional force between our hand and the glass prevents the glass from slipping from our hand. However, it is difficult to hold the glass of water when our hand is oily. This is because oil reduces the frictional force between our hand and the glass.
