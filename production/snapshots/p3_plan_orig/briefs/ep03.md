Living things grow (they get bigger and more complex), respond to changes in the environment, and reproduce so that their kind survives.

## Misconceptions to bust
- "Growing just means getting bigger." It also means becoming more complex.
- "Plants cannot respond." A plant bends toward light; the mimosa folds within seconds (do not say plants always respond slowly).
- "A kite or balloon 'responds'." Something pushes it; that is not a living thing responding.
- "Every egg hatches." Not all do, which is why some animals lay many eggs.
