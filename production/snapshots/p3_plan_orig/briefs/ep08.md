## Big idea
Objects are made of materials: wood, metal, ceramic, rubber, glass, plastic and fabric, and each material comes from its own source.

## Misconceptions to bust
- "An object and a material are the same thing." Spoon (object) vs metal (material).
- "A backpack is one object, so it is one material." One object can use many materials (lab 3.1).
- "Glass is made from plastic / ice." From sand.
- "Metal is one thing." Gold, iron, copper and aluminium are different metals.

## Exam wording box
1. Materials are used to make objects. Objects are things you use for different purposes.
2. Objects are made up of various types of materials: wood, metal, ceramic, rubber, glass, plastic and fabric.
3. Ceramic is a material made from clay. Glass is made from sand. Plastic is made in factories using oil that comes from deep under the ground.
4. Fabric is a material that can come from animals or plants.
