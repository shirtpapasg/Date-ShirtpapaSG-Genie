## Big idea
The electrical method makes an electromagnet (a wire coiled round an iron nail and connected to a battery) that works only while electricity flows; more turns of wire or more batteries make it stronger; use batteries only, not for long, and never the mains.

## Misconceptions to bust
- "An electromagnet stays a magnet after switching off." It works only while electricity flows.
- "Any rod works." Only a magnetic material (iron nail) can be magnetised.
- "More batteries is the only way." More turns of wire also works.
- Safety: batteries only, briefly, never the mains.
