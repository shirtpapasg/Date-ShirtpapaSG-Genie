## Misconceptions to bust
- "Plants eat sunlight / soil / are fed from a tray." Plants make their own food when there is light (keep the lab's line "a plant makes its own food").
- "A plant needs only water and air." All three, for plants too.
- "The plant is sad." It is wilting (a science word, not a feeling word).
- "The toy boat floats on water, so it needs water." Floating is not needing (lab 1.2 Part (b)).

## Exam wording box
1. Living things need water, food and air.
2. All living things need food. Food provides living things with energy.
3. Plants do not eat but they can make their own food when there is light.
4. Living things breathe in air that contains oxygen.

## Pause and predict
Q: Which does a plant need to survive? Only water / water and air only / water, food and air. **Answer: C.** It makes its own food, but it still needs food.
