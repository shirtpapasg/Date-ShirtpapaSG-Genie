## Big idea
Cycles are repeated patterns of change in living and non-living things (day and night; life cycles); a life cycle is a repeated pattern of change as a living thing grows over time and reproduces.

## Misconceptions to bust
- "Day and night happen because the Sun moves round the Earth." The Earth rotates.
- "A cycle has a start and an end." It repeats; there is no first or last stage (prepares EP 18).
- "Everything that changes is a cycle." A pop or a break happens once; a cycle repeats.
- "A teddy bear grows / has a life cycle." Non-living things do not grow.

## Exam wording box
1. Cycles are repeated patterns of change. They happen in living and non-living things.
2. The repeated change between day and night is caused by the rotation of the Earth. This rotation takes 24 hours.
3. A life cycle is a repeated pattern of change as a living thing grows over time and reproduces.
4. Life cycles ensure that living things of the same kind can continue to live on Earth.
