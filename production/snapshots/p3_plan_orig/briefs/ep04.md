## Big idea
Scientists classify living things by their similarities and differences into four main groups (plants, animals, fungi, bacteria); plants are flowering (seeds in fruits) or non-flowering (spores).

## Misconceptions to bust
- "No flower open today, so it is non-flowering." The group is decided by whether it CAN flower (grass, hibiscus).
- "Spores mean fungus." Ferns (plants) make spores too; the difference is that plants make their own food when there is light.
- "Seeds are made inside the flower." The flower develops into a fruit; the seeds are inside the fruit (book wording, not the lab's).
- "Leaves, roots and stems decide the group." They do not.

## Exam wording box
1. Classification is the grouping of things based on their similarities and differences.
2. Living things can be classified into four main groups — plants, animals, fungi and bacteria.
3. Flowering plants bear flowers. They reproduce from seeds. The flowers in flowering plants develop into fruits. The seeds are found in the fruits.
4. Non-flowering plants do not bear flowers. They reproduce from spores that may be found in spore bags.
