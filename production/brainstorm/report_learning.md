# Lens 2 (learning science, PSLE): "Lock It In" — summary of the Fable agent's report (19 Nov 2026)
- Each episode: one everyday Singapore puzzle pupils will likely get wrong → The Bet (1/2/3 fingers, 6 s ring timer, "clunk") → Flashback (spaced retrieval of an older episode) → first-person Journey → Wrong Idea, Busted (child's speech bubbles crossed out with evidence) → Name It (definition built from scenes; max 4 keywords) → Test It (fair test, results table, second lock-in reading data) → Build the Answer (3-box cause-effect chain; Heart fills box 1, viewer says 2-3; weak answer shown losing a mark) → one-picture recap, try at home, next Bet teaser, "Now you know!". Max 4 lock-in beats.
- Evaporation example "The Towel That Wouldn't Dry": HDB corridor, spread vs folded PE towel; misconceptions: water disappears / needs boiling or sun / wind makes it slower / kettle "steam" is vapour. Fan vs no-fan handkerchief table (50→38 g vs 50→46 g). 
- Pause answer: a timed beat helps if the child actually tries; make trying easy: closed 2-3 choice question, physical response, 5-7 s visible timer with sound, answer hidden during beat, pay-off addresses each choice, same routine every episode. Useless: rhetorical/vague questions, Heart talking over it, >4-5 beats.
- Classroom: 1-page sheet with 4 pause points (fingers up, tell your partner why; teacher holds 30 s). App: spaced quiz day 2/7/21, drag-the-boxes answer builder, choose-the-variable lab, wrong answers jump to the correcting "moment".
- Risks: showing wrong idea only as a crossed-out child's bubble, never Heart's voice; particles not formally in syllabus — exam words stay textbook; realistic data; routine staleness.
- Test: crossover with two classes and two topics, pre-test, post-test a week later (misconception MCQs, new-context open-ended, table reading); YouTube retention at each beat.
- Science check for synthesis: in "Build the Answer", larger exposed surface area means more water is in contact with the surrounding air so more evaporates at a time; avoid "gains heat faster" as the reason.
