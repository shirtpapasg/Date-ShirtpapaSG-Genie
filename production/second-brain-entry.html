🎙️ Narration voices: decided 10 Oct 2026

Engine: Kokoro TTS (free, offline, Apache 2.0), base voice af_heart ("Heart"), American English. Expressive version = storyteller pacing + pitch range widened with Praat (PSOLA).

Voice 3: "Heart, expressive" (DEFAULT for all YouTube videos)

  • Used for: P4 Science, Explained (all 22 episodes) and all future YouTube videos.
  • Speed 0.92 for statements, 0.86 for questions and exclamations.
  • 0.3 s beat between sentences; 0.55 s between lines; 1.0 s between scenes.
  • Pitch range widened 1.6× around her average (livelier rises and falls, same timbre).

Voice 4: "Heart, very expressive" (for FOUNDATION SCIENCE)

  • Used for: Foundation Science resources, for students who learn more slowly and need more support.
  • Same storyteller pacing as voice 3, pitch range widened 1.9× for extra warmth and emphasis.
  • Suggested extras for Foundation learners: longer pauses after each teaching point (try 0.8 s between lines, 1.4 s between scenes), shorter sentences, one idea per sentence, key words repeated.
  • Note: 1.9× is near the limit and can sound slightly warbly; listen-check and drop to 1.8× if needed.
