TheOneApp, Explained · Teacher reference

Which episode do I show today?

Every live episode for P3 to P6, sorted by the textbook chapter it teaches. Find the chapter or page you are on, then play the episode or jump straight to the part you need.

  1. Find your chapterPick the level, then look for the chapter you are teaching. For P3 and P6, type the page number, such as 56.
  2. Pick the episodeEach one covers about one lesson. Episodes marked A and B are two halves, for two lessons.
  3. Jump or link upOpen "Jump to a part" to start at one idea. The experiment number tells you the matching Activity Book page and School Lab bench in the app.
Before the lessonPlay the first minute as a hook. The opening question gets the class guessing.
During the lessonJump to the one part that explains the idea you are teaching, then pause and discuss.
After the lessonPlay it whole for revision, or set it as homework with the School Lab experiment.
