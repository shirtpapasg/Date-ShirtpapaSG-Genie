🎭 Character Voice Maker (new, keep for future use)

Kit: character-voice-kit (voice_maker.py, SKILL.md, samples). Free and offline: Kokoro TTS + Praat. Backup copy: GitHub shirtpapasg/Date-ShirtpapaSG-Genie, branch claude/cool-hypatia-v13e6t, folder production/character-voice-kit.

Recipe: blend Kokoro voices → speak → Praat "Change gender" (raises formants + pitch median, widens pitch range) → loudness −16 LUFS, 48 kHz. Plain pitch-shifting (rubberband) sounded robotic, so it is not used.
