## Settings for EVERY episode (important)

- **Audience:** choose **"Yes, it's made for kids."** The videos are aimed at 9–12-year-olds, and US law (COPPA) plus YouTube's own rules require this setting for child-directed videos. It turns off personalised ads and comments.
  - **The comment questions in the videos:** the close of each video asks viewers to "tell me in the comments". Made-for-kids videos have comments switched off. Either keep the line as a "think about it" prompt, or ask me to change future closes to "Tell your teacher or a friend…".
- **Visibility for review:** upload as **Unlisted**. Only people with the link can watch. Switch to **Public** after you approve.
- **Category:** Education. **Language:** English.
- **Altered or synthetic content:** answer **No**. The narration is a computer voice, but it doesn't imitate a real person and the videos are cartoon animations, so YouTube's disclosure for realistic content doesn't apply. You can still mention it openly in the description (included below).
- **Playlist:** create **"TheOneApp, Explained"** and add every episode to it.
